## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute-51-second single-modality infrared sensor clip flagged in the uploader title as a **"Hi-Res"** release variant, that AARO assesses is "likely derived from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform operating within the United States Central Command area of responsibility in 2019." The uploader-assigned title (per the DVIDS Title field) is "Hi-Res: [CALLSIGN] Observes UAP on 25SEP19 at 1715Z" — implying a capture at **25 September 2019 at 17:15 UTC** by a callsign-redacted U.S. military observer. The file was uploaded to a classified network in **November 2019**, ~6 weeks after the implied capture date — a clean short provenance gap (parallel to sibling PR097's October 2019 upload). The video description gives a seven-stage timeline: silent first 44 seconds; an area of contrast briefly transits the left side and exits at lower-left at 00:45–00:46; another silent gap 00:47–01:07; a new area of contrast enters from the left at 01:08–01:25 and is centred by sensor pan; sensor changes visual settings 01:26–01:45; **a second area of contrast joins from the bottom at 01:46–01:47**; at 01:47–01:55 the **screen flashes black** before tracking resumes, with **multiple areas of contrast entering and exiting the FOV**; from 04:00–04:51 the screen flashes black again before tracking an area of contrast.
