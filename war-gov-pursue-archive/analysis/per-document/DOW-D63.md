## Summary / Резюме

USCENTCOM long-endurance ISR sortie (21:04 mission, 17.3 IMINT hours, 15.3
[redacted] hours, 4 taskings prosecuted) launched from **OKAS** (Ali Al Salem
AB, Kuwait) at 012249Z on 1 October 2020, recovered to OKAS at 021953Z on
2 October. Operator unit `482 ATKS` / `432 AEW`, MAJCOM **AFCENT** (D61/D62
list ACC — clerical variation); AN/DAS-4 turret + **AH-BSWARIO** avionics
(different designator from D61/D62's `AH_GMESH`). Mission supported NAVCENT ISO
IRIN/IRGCN vessel characterisation, UAS activity, and pattern-of-life across
the Arabian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, and Gulf of Oman.

The sortie carried **5 GUARDCALLs from Iranian Air Defense** within 6 hours
(0727Z, 0854Z, 1122Z, 1236Z, 1315Z — three PROFESSIONAL, two DIRECTIVE) — the
**densest Iranian challenge cycle in the 2020 cluster**. All five received
standard call/response with no mission impact.

At **1829Z** (per Narrative), the FMV operator observed **"1X UAP"**, with
reference to OBSERVATION LINE 1. **The OBSERVATION block itself does not appear
in the available transcript** — the OCR output ends at page 8 of 8 with the
fifth GUARDCALL fully transcribed but the UAP OBSERVATION block apparently on
a later page that was not captured or not OCR'd. The Narrative line is the only
confirmed UAP signal preserved.

Other notable IMINT: 1x U/I aircraft on runway at Abu Musa Island Airfield
(1244Z); 1x U/I aircraft assessed as an **ATR 72-500** at Abu Musa Airfield
(1344Z); 1x possible Naser WAP docked IVO Bushehr IRIN boatyard (1657Z).
Heavy haze precluded IMINT analysis throughout the day.

**RU:** Длительный ISR-вылет 482 ATKS / 432 AEW (21:04, 17,3 IMINT, 4 задачи) с авиабазы OKAS 1–2 октября 2020 г. Самый высокий «гард-вызов»-цикл в кластере: **5 иранских вызовов ПВО за 6 часов** (три профессиональных, два директивных). В 1829Z оператор FMV наблюдал **«1 НЛО»** (единственная сохранившаяся строка нарратива); блок OBSERVATION с деталями (MGRS, кинематика, описание) **не вошёл в транскрипт** — OCR оборвался на странице 8 из 8 до блока OBSERVATION.
