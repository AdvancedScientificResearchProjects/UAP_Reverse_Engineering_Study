## Summary / Резюме

This is the **most heavily-redacted modern Department-of-War MISREP** in PURSUE
Release 01: the entire document body is masked under `1.4(a)` exemptions
*except* for the GENTEXT/UAP block. What survives is a single self-contained
narrative paragraph:

> **DURING RTB AT 1319Z, [redacted 1.4a] OBSERVED 1X UAP WHILE TRANSITING OVER
> 363453N 0255943E FLYING AT AN ALTITUDE OF 24,989 FT MSL AND SPEED OF 168 KTS.**

Plus a UAP description:

> **1X UAP DETECTED WITH DESCRIPTIVE INFORMATION SUCH AS BEING A TRIANGULAR
> AND METALLIC UAP.**

Both blocks are bracketed `(SECRET//NOFORN)`.

The phenomenology — a triangular, metallic-classified UAP observed at a
specific altitude and speed during return-to-base — places this case in a
narrow phenomenology slot: it is **the only "triangular and metallic" UAP
classification in the modern Department-of-War cluster**, and one of only two
"triangular" descriptors total (the other being [`DOW-D51`](DOW-D51.md)). The
altitude (24,989 ft MSL) and speed (168 KTS) are reported precisely, suggesting
these are sensor/system-derived values from the friendly aircraft's track on
the UAP rather than free-form operator estimates.

The friendly aircraft's flight profile (RTB at 24,989 ft / 168 KTS) is
consistent with an AFSOC U-28A Draco class long-loiter ISR aircraft on its
descent / transit phase home — the same airframe class that produced
[`DOW-D33`](DOW-D33.md), `DOW-D35`, and `DOW-D25` from Greek-island basing
patterns (LGLR / Larisa AB) in the same theatre.

What is *not* in the document: the date, year, season; the friendly aircraft
callsign, tail, MDS Type; the squadron, wing, ATO number, operation; the
sensor stack; the UAP propulsion / signatures / effects / observer-assessment
fields; any AARO disposition. The redaction-by-omission is exceptionally
aggressive — the release-cleared content is **only the UAP description and the
encounter narrative sentence**.

The geographic coordinate (Aegean Sea SE of Santorini) is the strongest single
inference anchor: it places the encounter in the **same theatre** as the
[`DOW-D33`](DOW-D33.md) / `DOW-D35` Aegean Oct 2023 cluster.

**RU:** Это **наиболее засекреченный современный MISREP** в PURSUE Release 01: всё тело документа скрыто под редакциями `1.4(a)`, кроме блока GENTEXT/UAP. Уцелело одно самодостаточное повествовательное предложение (RTB в 1319Z, наблюдение над координатами 36°34'53"N 25°59'43"E, высота 24 989 ft, скорость 168 KTS) и описание НЛО: «треугольный и металлический». Это **единственная классификация "треугольный и металлический" в современном кластере DoW** и один из двух треугольных дескрипторов в корпусе (второй — D51, гражданский свидетель). Высота и скорость даны с точностью, характерной для данных сенсорного трека, а не оценок оператора. Профиль полёта (RTB, 24 989 ft, 168 KTS) согласуется с долгоипящим ISR-воздушным судном класса U-28A Draco. Дата полностью засекречена; координаты помещают событие в Эгейское море (~20 морских миль к ENE от Санторини, Греция).
