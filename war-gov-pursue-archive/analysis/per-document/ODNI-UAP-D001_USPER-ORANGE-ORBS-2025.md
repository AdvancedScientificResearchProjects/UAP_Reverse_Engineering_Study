## Summary / Резюме

**EN:** A senior U.S. intelligence-community officer (USIC) authored a first-person narrative of a multi-hour helicopter encounter, in late 2025, with orange/yellow ovoid orbs over a U.S. weapons test range. Triggered by "loud thuds" in the mountains coincident with prior nights' UAP sightings, the officer departed a Joint Operations Center (JOC) with a colleague and two pilots, flew a low-altitude reconnaissance route, located a "large cave entrance with no visible end in sight", refuelled at a prepositioned tanker, and was directed by JOC to investigate radar hits in an area of prior UAP activity. Over the following hour the team observed: (i) a "super-hot" FLIR-imaged object that approached within ten feet of the helicopter, split into two, and accelerated out of NVG range; (ii) "countless orange orbs swarming in all directions" against a mountain backdrop; (iii) at 700 ft AGL hover, two large orange orbs with white/yellow centres flared up stationary just above the rotor disk, joined sequentially by a third and fourth below to form a "T" formation, all dimmed in reverse order and vanished after 10–15 seconds; (iv) the same orb-pattern reappeared **directly above fighter jets** that had launched on a training mission, "chasing" them, then dimmed sequentially as the jets transited and landed.

**RU:** Старший офицер разведсообщества США (USIC) составил нарратив от первого лица о многочасовом вертолётном наблюдении в конце 2025 оранжево-жёлтых овоидных сфер над американским испытательным полигоном. Триггер — «громкие удары» в горах, совпавшие по времени с UAP-наблюдениями предыдущих ночей. Команда: автор-офицер, коллега, два пилота. Маршрут — низковысотный «огибание рельефа», обнаружение «большой пещеры без видимого окончания», дозаправка на предпозиционированном танкере, перенаправление JOC на радарные засветки. За час: (i) «сверх-горячий» объект на FLIR подошёл к вертолёту на 3 метра, разделился надвое, ускорился вне диапазона ПНВ; (ii) «бесчисленные оранжевые сферы, роящиеся во всех направлениях»; (iii) на висении 700 ft AGL — две большие оранжевые сферы с бело-жёлтым центром, неподвижные над несущим винтом справа, к ним добавились третья и четвёртая ниже, сформировав «T-формацию», погасли в обратном порядке и исчезли через 10–15 секунд; (iv) тот же паттерн повторился **прямо над истребителями**, поднятыми по тревоге на тренировочный вылет, «преследуя» их и гасну в очерёдности по мере пролёта.

## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. **Which test range?** Geographic clues: mountains; large cave entrance; pre-positioned aerial-refuelling tanker for helicopter; FBI Western-US 2025 IR-photo connection. **Likely candidates: Nellis Test and Training Range (NV) or White Sands Missile Range (NM)**. The combination of mountain terrain + USIC senior officer + FBI Western-US IR series narrows to either Nevada or New Mexico — but the document does not explicitly state.
2. The "large cave entrance with no visible end" is mentioned and then dropped in the narrative — **was it subsequently entered**? Does a follow-on report exist?
3. The fighter-jet "training mission" coincidence — were the jets actually responding to the UAP cluster under operational cover of "training"?
4. Why is the narrative released by **ODNI** (not Air Force or DoD)? Suggests AARO/ODNI ownership of the broader incident package.
5. The "USPER Statement about UAP Sighting" in R01 — is it a *summary* version of this narrative, or a *different witness's* statement? Comparative analysis required.

## Notes / Замечания

- **This is the most narratively detailed first-person UAP testimony in the PURSUE corpus to date** — substantially richer than any of the R01 MISREPs, which use a constrained reporting-form syntax.
- The narrative voice and structure (chronological, low-jargon, threat-assessment-orientation) is consistent with a written first-person statement prepared for AARO or an Inspector-General review process, rather than a real-time operational report.
- Behaviour pattern observed — **stationary flaring orbs in geometric formation, sequential illumination, sequential dimming** — is consistent with multiple historical sightings across decades and continents (compare gershtein-archive Soviet "fiery flowers" cluster).
- The witness's deliberate non-photography is unusual for a modern intelligence officer with a phone; it is consistent with operational training that prioritises situational-awareness over evidence-collection in close-range encounters.
- The "we were virtually speechless" closing line is rhetorically powerful but the narrative does not include cited operational follow-up (debrief details redacted).
