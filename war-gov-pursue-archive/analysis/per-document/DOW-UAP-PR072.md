## Summary / Резюме

A 17-second clip from a commercial cellular-device rear-facing camera, recorded in March 2022 in the vicinity of **Karaganda International Airport, Kazakhstan**, and uploaded to a classified network in April 2023. Its uploader-defined title carries the **"ADMINISTRATIVE REVISION:"** prefix and references the structured intelligence product **IIR 1777 J0032 22** (Intelligence Information Report, originator unit code 1777, serial 0032, year 2022). AARO's video description discloses an unusual provenance flag: **"This media was digitally altered prior to its upload to a classified network, and is presented as received."** The active content runs 00:04–00:13 (~9 s) and shows "a luminous phenomenon with trails of diminishing brightness extending from the center" while the camera pans left and right and zooms in.

Заданное загрузчиком название несёт префикс **"АДМИНИСТРАТИВНАЯ РЕДАКЦИЯ:"** и ссылается на структурированный разведпродукт **IIR 1777 J0032 22** (Intelligence Information Report, код подразделения-источника 1777, серийный 0032, год 2022). PR072 is structurally exceptional in the R02 PR-cluster on **three independent dimensions**. (1) It is the **only PR-record with explicit Kazakhstan / Soviet-bloc geography** — every other R02 PR-record locates within CENTCOM AOR (Iran / Syria / Iraq / Arabian Sea), INDOPACOM AOR (East China Sea), or NORTHCOM AOR (CONUS / Lake Huron). Kazakhstan is **post-Soviet Central Asia**, geographically and historically distinct from any other PURSUE locality. The choice of **Karaganda** is significant: Karaganda sits on the southern approach to **Sary Shagan** (the legendary Soviet ABM / directed-energy weapons test range covered by `CIA-UAP-D001` in R02) and is ~600 km north of **Baikonur Cosmodrome** — both critical USSR-legacy aerospace facilities. (2) It is the **only PR-record in R02 explicitly flagged as "digitally altered" prior to classified-network ingest** — i.e. AARO is informing the reader that the file's pixels were modified by an unknown hand before AARO ever received it. This is a fundamental chain-of-custody collapse. (3) The **"ADMINISTRATIVE REVISION:"** prefix indicates the underlying IIR was re-issued with revisions; AARO is publishing the revised IIR's associated video, but neither the original IIR nor the revised IIR text is in the tranche — only the video and AARO's hedged description.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Only Soviet-bloc PR-record in R02 / Единственная советско-блочная PR-запись в R02:** PR072 is geographically and historically isolated within the R02 PR-cluster, which otherwise concentrates on CENTCOM / INDOPACOM / NORTHCOM AORs. The Kazakhstan setting connects PR072 thematically to the entirely separate Soviet-aerospace strand running through PURSUE (DOS-KAZ-1994, DOS-TKM-2004, DOS-TBI-2001, CIA-UAP-D001 Sary Shagan, DOW-1955-AZERBAIJAN Trans-Caucasus). This is the strongest single argument for cross-linking the PURSUE Russian / Soviet thread to the Russian-language ASRP archives (chernobrov, gershtein) at the document level.
- **"ADMINISTRATIVE REVISION" prefix — provenance flag / "АДМИНИСТРАТИВНАЯ РЕДАКЦИЯ" — флаг происхождения:** the prefix indicates this is a **re-issued** IIR. Without sight of either the original or the revised IIR text, the revision's substance is unknown. The combination of (revised IIR + uploaded April 2023 + digitally altered + 13-month delay from event date) is a four-way provenance compromise unmatched by any other PR-record.
- **Digital-alteration disclosure is unprecedented for the PR-cluster / Раскрытие цифрового изменения беспрецедентно для PR-кластера:** AARO routinely caveats chain-of-custody but rarely states outright that a released file's pixels have been modified by an unknown party before AARO ingest. This is the most aggressive provenance-warning AARO has issued in R02 and substantially weakens any forensic-analytic value of the released bitstream.
- **Karaganda airport + March 2022 spaceflight context / Аэропорт Караганды + контекст пилотируемой космонавтики марта 2022:** March 2022 was operationally significant at Baikonur — Soyuz MS-21 (carrying 3 cosmonauts) launched 18 March 2022 from Baikonur to ISS during the high-tension early weeks of the Russia–Ukraine war; multiple Russian launch campaigns and ground tests occurred in the surrounding window. A bright luminous trail seen from Karaganda in March 2022 has a substantial prosaic-launch prior probability that any analytic treatment must address before assigning weight to the "UAP" framing.
- **Boilerplate / Стандартный текст:** R02 51-records boilerplate paragraph present (March 6, 2026 House Representatives request, AARO collection from classified network, CoC caveat). The PR072 provenance is materially weaker than R02 baseline due to disclosed digital alteration and administrative revision combined.
