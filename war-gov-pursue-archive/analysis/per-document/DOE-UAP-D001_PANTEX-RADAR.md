## Summary / Резюме

**EN:** Pantex Plant (the U.S. nuclear-weapons assembly/disassembly facility outside Amarillo, Texas, operated by CNS LLC for DOE-NNSA) filed an "Unidentified Object Incident Report" documenting an image captured from a **Ground Surveillance Radar Tower**. The 6-page bundle includes the incident summary, the original Pantex radar tower imagery, and "Sandia National Labs Enhanced Images of the Object" — i.e., Sandia performed image post-processing on the Pantex source frames. Body details that would describe the object are redacted under **(b)(3) UCNI**.

**RU:** Завод Pantex (объект сборки/разборки ядерных боеприпасов США в окрестностях Амарилло, Техас; оператор CNS LLC по контракту с DOE-NNSA) составил «Отчёт об инциденте с неопознанным объектом» по изображению с башни **наземного радара наблюдения**. Бюллетень 6 страниц включает резюме инцидента, исходные кадры с башни Pantex и «улучшенные Sandia National Labs изображения объекта» — то есть Sandia применили post-processing к исходным кадрам. Содержательные описания объекта закрыты редакциями (b)(3) UCNI.

## Notes / Замечания

- File is small (~160 KB PDF) but **information density is high** — the actual evidentiary content is the *visual imagery*, not the textual narrative. Direct PDF visual inspection is essential.
- Pantex is the **only** U.S. nuclear-weapons assembly/disassembly facility. Its UAP incidents are categorically more sensitive than perimeter incidents at most defense sites.
- The chain "**radar tower observation → Sandia image enhancement → DOE UCNI report → PURSUE release**" is itself a procedural data point about the DOE incident-handling pipeline.
- The fact that DOE is contributing UAP records to PURSUE at all is itself a precedent. DOE has historically declined to release UFO/UAP material under its own categorical exclusions.
