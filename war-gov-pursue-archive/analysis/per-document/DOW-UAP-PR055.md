## Summary / Резюме

A 47-second infrared-sensor clip from a U.S. military platform in CENTCOM AOR, dated November 2020 by the uploader and labelled by them as a "Spherical UAP over AFG in and out of clouds 23 Nov 2020." Of all PR records in Release 02, PR055 is among the most heavily user-annotated: it contains three explicit black-screen text cards inserted by the original uploader ("zoomed in", "sharpened, zoomed motion tracked contrast enhanced slow to 60% speed", "original video"), wrapping a deliberate replay sequence. The actual UAP footage spans only roughly 23 of the 47 seconds (00:03–00:10, 00:12–00:28 replay, and 00:30–00:47), with the rest taken up by annotated interstitials.

**RU:** 47-секундный клип инфракрасного сенсора с военной платформы США в зоне ответственности CENTCOM, датированный загрузчиком ноябрём 2020 и помеченный им как «Сферический НЛО над AFG в облаках и из облаков 23 ноября 2020». Из всех PR-записей Release 02 PR055 — одна из наиболее активно аннотированных пользователем: содержит три явных интерстициала с чёрным экраном, вставленных оригинальным загрузчиком («zoomed in», «sharpened, zoomed motion tracked contrast enhanced slow to 60% speed», «original video»), обрамляющих умышленный реплей. Фактические UAP-кадры занимают лишь около 23 из 47 секунд (00:03–00:10, реплей 00:12–00:28 и 00:30–00:47), остальное приходится на аннотированные интерстициалы.

AARO acknowledges this directly: "This media was digitally altered prior to its upload to a classified network, and is presented as received." PR055 is therefore not a raw sensor capture but a user-curated mini-edit prepared for analyst consumption — making the lineage commentary unusually visible. The "Spherical UAP" label is the uploader's vocabulary, not AARO's; AARO's prose stays with the disciplined "area of contrast" formulation throughout the timeline.

**RU:** AARO прямо это признаёт: «Этот материал был цифрово изменён до загрузки в закрытую сеть и представлен в полученном виде». PR055 — это не сырой сенсорный захват, а пользовательский мини-монтаж, подготовленный для аналитика, благодаря чему происхождение материала необычно прозрачно. Ярлык «Spherical UAP» — словарь загрузчика, не AARO; проза AARO в описании таймлайна неизменно сохраняет дисциплинированную формулировку «area of contrast» («область контраста»).

The 23 Nov 2020 date is interesting in the CENTCOM context: by late 2020 the US presence in Afghanistan was already in drawdown (CSTC-A reduced footprint, ISR orbits constrained), so an Afghanistan-airspace UAP capture in this period is operationally non-trivial. Without a paired MISREP it remains an orphan, but the user-supplied date is one of the most specific in the R02 PR series.

**RU:** Дата 23 ноября 2020 интересна в контексте CENTCOM: к концу 2020 присутствие США в Афганистане уже сокращалось (уменьшенный footprint CSTC-A, ограниченные ISR-орбиты), поэтому захват UAP в воздушном пространстве Афганистана в этот период операционно нетривиален. Без парного MISREP запись остаётся сиротой, но указанная пользователем дата — одна из наиболее конкретных в серии PR R02.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Most heavily user-annotated PR in R02 first wave:** PR055 contains three uploader-inserted black-screen text cards labelling the processing chain ("zoomed in" / "sharpened, motion-tracked, slow to 60% speed" / "original video"). This level of visible curation is rare in the PURSUE corpus and turns the clip into a small analytic product rather than a raw sensor capture.
- **Afghanistan 2020 attribution is operationally non-trivial:** by November 2020 US forces in Afghanistan were already in drawdown; persistent ISR orbits with the bandwidth to spot, track, and re-process a "spherical UAP in/out of clouds" event were thinning. The date and location together make PR055 one of the more operationally specific orphans in the R02 PR series.
- **Uploader vocabulary leakage:** the word "Spherical" appears only in the uploader-defined title, not in AARO's description timeline (which holds to "area of contrast"). AARO carries the uploader title intact rather than rewriting it — a documentation choice that preserves provenance but blurs the line between operator language and AARO assessment.
- **Real footage duration ≈ 23 s of the 47-s file:** roughly half of the released clip is annotated interstitial. Consumers measuring "minutes of UAP footage released" should not credit PR055 with a full 47 s.
- **DVIDS Location anomaly:** the "United States" location field contradicts AARO's prose AFG / CENTCOM attribution. Likely a systematic upload-side default in the war.gov DVIDS pipeline; treat AARO description prose as authoritative for incident geography across the R02 PR series.
- **Boilerplate / disclaimer:** standard AARO release boilerplate and non-analysis disclaimer present.
