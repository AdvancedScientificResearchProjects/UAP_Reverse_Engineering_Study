## Summary / Резюме

This is the **single civilian-witness UAP record** in the modern Department-of-War
PURSUE Release 01 tranche. The underlying observation is a March 2023 night-time
sighting **"hovering stationary above or near a national security facility for
approximately three minutes"**, reported by a single civilian using a
**personal cellular device** to capture media. The reporter described a
**"large blue featureless triangular object with a solid, unwavering silhouette
emitting powerful 'whitish blue' light from multiple points along its perimeter"**.
The total observation lasted approximately **eight minutes**, of which the
final portion saw the object move to **"a position higher in their field of
view"** characterised as **"backing up" in a "jerking" or "jumping" manner
inconsistent with 'smooth' jet propulsion"**.

The email thread itself documents an **OUSD(I&S) ↔ AFOSI Counterintelligence**
classification-review request: the OUSD(I&S) Information Disclosure Analyst
needs the source IIR's UAP narrative summary downgraded from S//NF to
UNCLASSIFIED for inclusion in a downstream product. The AFOSI CI Collections
and Operations PM — who owns the source IIR — initially treats the request as a
formal declassification (which "requires AFOSI Commander signature so I'm
exploring options akin to a security review"), then resolves the issue by
processing it as a **derivative classification review**, concurring with
UNCLASSIFIED release.

The phenomenological description preserved in the thread is the most detailed
civilian-witness record in the corpus — and one of only two records in the
modern Department-of-War cluster to use the **"triangular"** shape descriptor
(the other being [`DOW-D54`](DOW-D54.md)). The reporter's negative
descriptors are equally interesting: **"didn't think"** the object was a drone;
**did not seem to travel along a defined flight path**; did not appear to
possess **"photograph or data collection capabilities"**; did not emit a
**"vapor trail"**; did not demonstrate **"cloaking capabilities"**.

What the document does *not* contain: the exact location, the identity of the
"national security facility", any details about the AFOSI / NORTHCOM
investigation, the IIR identifier, follow-on radar / IR cross-correlation,
AARO disposition, or any link to a PR-series video.

**RU:** Это **единственная запись с гражданским свидетелем** в современном трансе PURSUE Release 01. Наблюдение в марте 2023 года (ночь, ~8 минут): гражданский наблюдатель с личного сотового устройства зафиксировал «большой синий бесформенный треугольный объект с твёрдым, невозмутимым силуэтом, излучающий мощный "бело-синий" свет из нескольких точек по периметру». Объект ~3 минуты завис над объектом национальной безопасности, затем «попятился» «дёрганым» движением, несовместимым с реактивной тягой. Письмо документирует процедуру понижения секретности IIR через OUSD(I&S) ↔ AFOSI CI: первоначально трактовалась как полное рассекречивание (требует подписи командира AFOSI), переквалифицирована в производный классификационный обзор и одобрена как несекретная. Феноменология наиболее детальная среди гражданских записей корпуса; точное местоположение и объект национальной безопасности засекречены.
