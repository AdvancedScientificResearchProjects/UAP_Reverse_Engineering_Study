## Summary / Резюме

A 39-second infrared sensor clip — **the first U.S. Coast Guard-attributed record in the PURSUE corpus** — uploaded with the title fragment "USCG C-144 Tyndall UAP 2 TIC TAC IR hot 24 April 2024". The uploader explicitly labels the target morphology as **TIC TAC** (the same descriptor used for the USS Nimitz 2004 FLIR1 video) and records the IR polarity as **hot** (target hotter than background). AARO confirms USCG attribution, 2024 timeframe, Southeastern United States, and IR sensor — but does **not** in its own narrative endorse the "TIC TAC" morphology word; AARO's description uses the conservative "area of contrast" formulation throughout.

**RU:** Тридцатидевятисекундный клип инфракрасного сенсора — **первая в корпусе PURSUE запись, приписанная Береговой охране США** — загруженный с фрагментом названия «USCG C-144 Tyndall UAP 2 TIC TAC IR hot 24 April 2024». Загрузчик явно маркирует морфологию цели как **TIC TAC** (тот же дескриптор, что использовался для видео FLIR1 USS Nimitz 2004) и фиксирует ИК-полярность как **hot** (цель горячее фона). AARO подтверждает атрибуцию USCG, временной интервал 2024, юго-восток США и ИК-сенсор — но в своём нарративе **не** одобряет морфологическое слово «TIC TAC»; описание AARO повсюду использует консервативную формулировку «область контраста».

The phenomenology AARO documents is a 32-second continuous sensor track: the gimballed IR sensor pans persistently to keep one area of contrast in FOV from 00:00 through 00:32, before panning left at 00:33 and losing the target off the right edge of the frame. The 32-second continuous track is significantly longer than most R01/R02 PR-clip events, which more often consist of multi-second transits. PR065 is the **second of two paired Tyndall 2024 clips** (counterpart `PR066` is "UAP 1"), and the pair together should be read as a single encounter event with two recorded segments — uploader's "1" and "2" numbering implies temporal order.

**RU:** Феноменология, документированная AARO, — 32-секундная непрерывная сенсорная проводка: подвесной ИК-сенсор настойчиво панорамирует, удерживая одну область контраста в поле зрения с 00:00 по 00:32, прежде чем повернуть влево в 00:33 и потерять цель за правым краем кадра. 32-секундная непрерывная проводка значительно дольше большинства событий PR-клипов R01/R02, которые чаще состоят из секундных транзитов. ## Notes / Замечания

- **First USCG record in PURSUE corpus:** PR065 + PR066 together open the USCG sub-cluster in PURSUE — no Coast Guard-attributed records exist in R01. This is structurally significant: the PURSUE corpus had been entirely USAF/USN/USMC/Army-attributed before R02, and the USCG addition extends the sensor-platform diversity into maritime fixed-wing coastal patrol. Note that the **HC-144A Ocean Sentry** is the most likely C-144 variant; CN-235-class airframe with AN/AAS-52 turret.
- **Uploader's TIC TAC label is a deliberate Nimitz echo:** "TIC TAC" is not a generic shape word — it is the specific morphology descriptor canonised by the USS Nimitz 2004 FLIR1 video and reused in 2017 NYT coverage. The uploader's choice to use the same word, combined with "IR hot" (consistent with the Nimitz video's polarity), is a deliberate claim that this Tyndall observation matches the Nimitz phenomenology. AARO declines to endorse this framing.
- **32-second continuous track is unusually long:** most R01/R02 PR-cluster events are sub-10-second transits or multi-second contrast appearances. A 32-second uninterrupted sensor-pan track on one target is closer in duration to the Nimitz FLIR1 video (~1 minute) than to the typical R02 PR clip. This duration alone makes PR065 phenomenologically more analyser-friendly than most of the R02 cluster.
- **Track loss is operator-side, not target-evasion:** AARO is explicit that the sensor panned left at 00:33, causing the target to leave the FOV. This is **not** a target-acceleration / evasion event — it is gimbal operator action. Differs phenomenologically from the "lock-loss to acceleration" framing common in 2017–2020 Navy releases.
- **Boilerplate / Стандартный текст:** R02 51-records boilerplate paragraph present (March 6, 2026 House Representatives request, AARO collection from classified network, CoC caveat).
