## Summary / Резюме

A 46-second infrared sensor clip whose uploader-defined title states: **"USAF ANG F-16C (callsign [CALLSIGN]) Shoots Down UAP over Lake Huron with [Weapon System], 12 Feb 2023"** — directly identifying the encounter as the publicly-acknowledged Lake Huron shootdown of 12 February 2023, executed by an Air National Guard F-16C as the fourth NORAD engagement in the Feb 2023 "high-altitude object" sequence. AARO confirms only that the clip is "likely derived from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform operating within the United States Northern Command area of responsibility in 2023" and was uploaded to a classified network in February 2023 — i.e. **within days** of the engagement.

The video description is operationally explicit. At 00:11 the sensor focuses on an area of contrast at the centre of its FOV (track established). At 00:20 the footage **"appears to depict a kinetic interaction between two distinct areas of contrast, with the initial subject of the footage fragmenting in a radial displacement pattern that suggests a high-energy event"** — i.e. the impact and breakup of the target by an inbound weapon, with debris radiating from the impact point. This is AARO's most operationally-committed video description in the entire PR-cluster: the words *"kinetic interaction"*, *"fragmenting"*, *"radial displacement"*, and *"high-energy event"* together describe a weapons engagement with target destruction. AARO retains its standard non-analysis disclaimer immediately after this paragraph, but the description content itself is unambiguous.

AARO подтверждает лишь, что клип "вероятно получен с инфракрасного сенсора военной платформы США, работавшей в зоне ответственности Северного командования США в 2023 году" и был загружен в закрытую сеть в феврале 2023 — т.е. **в течение нескольких дней** после поражения.

Это самое оперативно-обязывающее описание видео AARO во всём PR-кластере: слова *"кинетическое взаимодействие"*, *"фрагментируется"*, *"радиальное смещение"* и *"высокоэнергетическое событие"* вместе описывают применение оружия с уничтожением цели. PR071 is the **single most operationally significant record in PURSUE Release 02**, and almost certainly the most significant single video across the entire combined R01+R02 corpus. It is the **only** PURSUE record in which a US fighter aircraft engages and destroys an unidentified object. Crucially, the underlying event is **independently corroborated by the public record**: President Biden addressed the Feb 2023 NORAD shootdowns in his 16 February 2023 White House remarks, NORTHCOM/NORAD held public briefings on 12–14 February 2023 (GEN Glen VanHerck), and the **Lake Huron object** specifically was confirmed by DoD as engaged by a USAF F-16C using an AIM-9X Sidewinder air-to-air missile (first AIM-9X missed; second AIM-9X hit). The object was characterised by US officials as "octagonal structure with strings hanging off it" at ~20,000 ft, distinct from the Chinese surveillance balloon shot down over the Atlantic off South Carolina on 4 February. The Feb 2023 sequence — China balloon (4 Feb) → Alaska object (10 Feb, F-22) → Yukon object (11 Feb, F-22 in Canadian airspace, US Air Force engagement) → **Lake Huron object (12 Feb, F-16C)** — was the most concentrated weapons-active NORAD engagement of unidentified airborne objects in the post-Cold-War era.

**RU:** PR071 — **самая оперативно значимая запись в PURSUE Релиз 02**, и почти наверняка самое значимое отдельное видео во всём объединённом корпусе R01+R02. Это **единственная** запись PURSUE, в которой американский истребитель поражает и уничтожает неопознанный объект. Что критически важно, исходное событие **независимо подтверждено публичным реестром**: Президент Байден обратился к февральским 2023 сбитиям NORAD в выступлении в Белом доме 16 февраля 2023, NORTHCOM/NORAD провели публичные брифинги 12–14 февраля 2023 (GEN Glen VanHerck), а **объект над озером Гурон** был конкретно подтверждён МО как поражённый F-16C ВВС США с использованием ракеты «воздух-воздух» AIM-9X Sidewinder (первая AIM-9X промахнулась; вторая AIM-9X попала). Объект был охарактеризован американскими официальными лицами как «восьмиугольная конструкция со свисающими стропами» на высоте ~20 000 футов, отличный от китайского разведывательного шара, сбитого над Атлантикой у Южной Каролины 4 февраля. Февральская 2023 последовательность — китайский шар (4 фев.) → объект над Аляской (10 фев., F-22) → объект над Юконом (11 фев., F-22 в канадском воздушном пространстве, поражение ВВС США) → **объект над озером Гурон (12 фев., F-16C)** — была самым концентрированным боевым задействованием NORAD против неопознанных воздушных объектов в эпоху после Холодной войны.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Corpus-unique weapons engagement / Уникальное в корпусе применение оружия:** PR071 is the **only PURSUE record in either R01 or R02** depicting a kinetic engagement of an unidentified target by a US fighter aircraft. Every other PR-record shows tracking, observation, lock-and-loss patterns — none show weapons release and target destruction. This makes PR071 a category-of-one record for downstream classification (corpus-wide it is "Class K — Kinetic engagement", a category that R02 *just established*).
- **Public-record corroboration is dispositive / Корроборация публичным реестром решающая:** unlike most PR-records — which are uploader-attributed, AARO-hedged, and lack independent corroboration — PR071's underlying event is one of the **best-publicly-documented UAP-adjacent military actions in US history**: White House podium remarks by the President, three days of NORAD/NORTHCOM press briefings, FAA NOTAM records, recovery-search-and-debris operations on Lake Huron (no debris definitively recovered), Canadian RCAF coordination on the prior-day Yukon shootdown. This is why PR071 earns the ✅ CORROBORATED verdict on the shootdown fact, despite the AARO boilerplate's standard CoC caveat.
- **Operationally-committed AARO language / Оперативно-обязывающая лексика AARO:** AARO's video description for PR071 contains the most operationally-direct vocabulary in any PR-record description: *"kinetic interaction"*, *"distinct areas of contrast"*, *"fragmenting"*, *"radial displacement"*, *"high-energy event"*. This is a markedly different register from the agency's standard "area of contrast" hedge — and the AARO disclaimer paragraph immediately after the description reads as a perfunctory legalism rather than as a substantive interpretive boundary.
- **Engagement physics consistent with AIM-9X kinematics / Физика поражения согласуется с кинематикой AIM-9X:** ~9 second interval between track establishment (00:11) and intercept (00:20) is consistent with a short-range IR-homing missile shot from co-altitude / co-airspace geometry; the AIM-9X has ~Mach 2.5 terminal velocity and 18+ km kinematic envelope. The "two distinct areas of contrast" framing is consistent with the warhead's plume / body being IR-visible against the cold Lake Huron background as it converges on the target. First-missile-miss / second-missile-hit dynamics widely reported in Feb 2023 are *not* visible in this single 46-second clip — implying the released file captures only the successful second shot.
- **CALLSIGN / weapon-system redaction asymmetry / Асимметрия редакции CALLSIGN / системы оружия:** AIM-9X identification is in the public record; the redaction is policy-conservative rather than secrecy-protective. The CALLSIGN redaction is more substantive — the engaging pilot's specific squadron / tail number, while traceable by determined OSINT, is not on the public record at the named-individual level.
- **Boilerplate / Стандартный текст:** R02 51-records boilerplate paragraph present (March 6, 2026 House Representatives request, AARO collection from classified network, CoC caveat). The CoC caveat sits awkwardly against the dispositive public-record corroboration of the underlying event — i.e. AARO maintains its standard chain-of-custody hedge even for an event the US Government publicly executed and announced.
- **Claim 6 verdict refinement / Уточнение вердикта для заявления 6:** Claim 6 marked 🟧 (analyst inference) rather than ✅ — the underlying Lake Huron shootdown is public record (Biden remarks 16 Feb 2023, NORTHCOM briefings, AIM-9X identification), but the specific "two converging IR contrast sources = AIM-9X-target intercept" interpretation is analyst inference from text-only AARO description; no raw video frame analysis was performed in this card.
