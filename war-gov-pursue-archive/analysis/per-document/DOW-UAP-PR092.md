## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute 52-second infrared sensor clip from a U.S. military platform in USCENTCOM AOR, dated **08 August 2020** by the original uploader. The released file is one of the longer R02 PR clips and shows a sustained sensor-operator engagement with a single area of contrast: the contrast feature enters from the lower-right at 00:48, briefly exits at 00:52–00:53, is reacquired by a panning sensor at 00:54, and is then **tracked continuously for ~4 minutes** (01:09 onward) through multiple zoom-in / zoom-out cycles and one explicit "visual settings" change at 04:08. The first 47 seconds of the file are blank.

Опубликованный файл — один из более длинных PR-клипов R02 и показывает устойчивое взаимодействие сенсорного оператора с единичной областью контраста: контрастная особенность входит из нижнего-правого в 00:48, ненадолго выходит в 00:52–00:53, переподхватывается панорамирующим сенсором в 00:54 и затем **непрерывно сопровождается ~4 минуты** (с 01:09) через несколько циклов наезда/отъезда и одно явное изменение «визуальных настроек» в 04:08. Первые 47 секунд файла пусты.

PR092 is analytically significant for two reasons. First, the **8 August 2020** date pushes the boundary of the previously observed Aug–Oct 2020 sustained CENTCOM cluster pattern (visible in R02 PR siblings and reinforced by AARO's later batch of October 2020 frames) about three weeks earlier into August, broadening the cluster envelope. Second, the **continuous ~4-minute track is unusually long for a PR-tier clip** — most R02 PR releases run under 60 seconds, and the only longer-form continuous engagements in the PR cluster involve uploader-curated edits (e.g. PR055). PR092's timeline reads as a single uninterrupted sensor take with operator zoom and modality adjustments, not as a stitched compilation.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Longest sustained single-target PR track surveyed in R02 so far:** PR092's continuous ~3.5-minute engagement (00:54–04:25) is substantially longer than the typical sub-60-second PR clip and exceeds the curated 47-second PR055. If real (not stitched), it implies either auto-track lock maintained over thousands of frames or a deliberate manual track by a tasked operator.
- **Cluster envelope expansion:** the 8 August 2020 date pushes the previously dense Aug–Oct 2020 CENTCOM cluster about three weeks earlier into August, suggesting the observed phenomenology was not strictly an autumn-2020 phenomenon but began earlier in the summer.
- **No black-screen interstitials, no uploader text cards:** unlike PR055, the timeline reads as a raw sensor take with operator zoom and modality changes only. This is the cleaner R02 PR format — closer to R01's IR-only B-roll convention than to R02's heavily curated mini-edits.
- **Identical "CALLSIGN CALLSIGN" double redaction in title:** the doubled bracket placeholders in the title likely indicate two separate operator/platform call signs were masked — i.e. the original title named both the aircraft callsign and the reporting unit. The doubled redaction is a stylistic marker AARO has not used in most other PR titles.
- **R02 boilerplate / disclaimer:** standard March 6, 2026 House Representatives request preamble, AARO classified-network sourcing, chain-of-custody caveat, and non-analysis disclaimer all present.
