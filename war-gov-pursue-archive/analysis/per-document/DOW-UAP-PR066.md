## Summary / Резюме

A 48-second infrared sensor clip — the **paired companion to PR065** and **the "1" of the two-clip USCG Tyndall encounter sequence**. Uploader title "USCG C-144 Tyndall UAP 1 TIC TAC IR hot 24 April 2024" mirrors PR065 exactly except for the sequence number. AARO confirms USCG attribution, 2024 timeframe, Southeastern United States, and IR sensor. The phenomenology AARO documents is **two distinct contrast-feature appearances** in the same clip: (1) 00:09–00:15 a contrast appears upper-right, sensor does **not** pan to track, and it exits off the left side; (2) 00:33–00:48 a contrast enters from lower-right, exits lower-left, sensor pans left but **is unable to track** the contrast.

**RU:** Сорокавосьмисекундный клип инфракрасного сенсора — **парный спутник PR065** и **«1» из двухклиповой последовательности встречи USCG Tyndall**. Название загрузчика «USCG C-144 Tyndall UAP 1 TIC TAC IR hot 24 April 2024» точно зеркалит PR065 за исключением порядкового номера. AARO подтверждает атрибуцию USCG, временной интервал 2024, юго-восток США и ИК-сенсор. Феноменология, документированная AARO, — **два различных появления контрастных особенностей** в одном клипе: (1) 00:09–00:15 контраст появляется в верхней-правой части, сенсор **не** панорамирует для отслеживания, и он уходит влево; (2) 00:33–00:48 контраст входит снизу-справа, уходит снизу-слева, сенсор поворачивает влево, но **не может отследить** контраст.

PR066 is phenomenologically **richer** than its sibling PR065 in two ways: (a) it records **two separate contrast appearances** rather than one continuous track; (b) the explicit "sensor pans to the left, but is unable to track" wording at 00:33–00:48 is a rare AARO acknowledgment that the **target's kinematic exceeded the gimbal's slew rate** — this is the kind of formulation that, in 2004 Nimitz analysis, was used to argue for non-prosaic acceleration. PR066's "unable to track" wording is the **most explicit operator-platform tracking failure** documented in the AARO video descriptions of the R02 PR-cluster so far, and is the strongest single piece of phenomenological substance in the USCG Tyndall pair.

**RU:** PR066 феноменологически **богаче** своего сиблинга PR065 в двух отношениях: (a) фиксирует **два отдельных появления контраста** вместо одной непрерывной проводки; (b) явная формулировка «сенсор поворачивает влево, но не может отследить» в 00:33–00:48 — редкое признание AARO, что **кинематика цели превзошла угловую скорость поворота подвеса** — формулировка, которая в анализе Nimitz 2004 использовалась для аргументации не-обыденного ускорения. ## Notes / Замечания

- **Paired with PR065 — read as one encounter:** PR065 (32-s continuous track) and PR066 (two discrete appearances + tracking failure) together form a single 24 April 2024 USCG Tyndall encounter event. The two clips should always be cross-referenced; isolated reading of either misses the encounter-level structure. Uploader's "1" / "2" implies PR066 is the earlier segment.
- **Tracking-failure language is the phenomenological core:** "sensor pans to the left, but is unable to track the area of contrast" is unusually direct for AARO's normally hedged Video Descriptions. It is **not** equivalent to "lost lock" — it's a statement that the gimbal hit a slew-rate limit. This is the type of statement that, in Nimitz / GO FAST / GIMBAL analyses, was foregrounded by ufology / dismissed by skeptics as parallax-induced apparent motion. The same debate applies here.
- **First-USCG-record significance / Значимость первой записи USCG:** PR065 + PR066 extend the PURSUE sensor-platform diversity into USCG HC-144A maritime patrol — the first time the corpus includes a non-DoD federal sensor. USCG operates under DHS in peacetime, DoN in wartime — the chain-of-custody for USCG-originated UAP footage is structurally different from USAF/USN releases, and PR065/PR066 establish a precedent for that route.
- **Tyndall geography is operationally plausible:** Tyndall AFB has had heavy 2023–2024 F-35 / 325th Fighter Wing reconstitution traffic post-Hurricane Michael, plus Eglin AFB nearby, plus the Gulf Test Range W-470 / W-151 warning areas — high baseline aerial activity. The phenomenological prior probability is shifted toward test-asset / training / range-target explanations more than for, say, the Persian Gulf 2022 cluster.
- **Boilerplate / Стандартный текст:** R02 51-records boilerplate paragraph present (March 6, 2026 House Representatives request, AARO collection from classified network, CoC caveat).
