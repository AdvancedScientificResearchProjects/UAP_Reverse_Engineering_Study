## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute-54-second infrared-sensor clip from a U.S. military platform in CENTCOM AOR on 5 September 2020 — **the temporal anchor** of the September 2020 CENTCOM sub-cluster (PR087 precedes PR085 by 11 days and PR084 by 12 days). Unlike PR084 (single 2-second event) and PR085 (single sustained ~3-minute track), PR087 documents **three discrete repeating transit events** with near-identical kinematic signature: at 01:23–01:26, 02:53–02:56, and 04:22–04:26, an area of contrast "transits the screen, entering from the bottom and exiting the top of the frame." Each event lasts 3–4 seconds; total event-of-interest time is ~10 s of 294 s (~3.4 %).

**RU:** 4-минутно-54-секундный клип инфракрасного сенсора с военной платформы США в зоне ответственности CENTCOM 5 сентября 2020 — **временной якорь** сентябрьского 2020 CENTCOM подкластера (PR087 предшествует PR085 на 11 дней и PR084 на 12 дней). В отличие от PR084 (одно 2-секундное событие) и PR085 (один устойчивый ~3-минутный трек), PR087 документирует **три отдельных повторяющихся транзитных события** с почти идентичной кинематической сигнатурой: в 01:23–01:26, 02:53–02:56 и 04:22–04:26 область контраста «пересекает экран, входя снизу и выходя в верхней части кадра». Каждое событие длится 3–4 секунды; общее время событий интереса — ~10 с из 294 с (~3,4 %).

The three events are **uniformly spaced ~90 seconds apart** (01:24 → 02:54 → 04:24 = 90 s + 90 s), with **identical bottom→top traversal direction**. This is the most repetitive transit pattern in the September 2020 sub-cluster and arguably one of the most structurally suggestive in the R02 PR set: a target population traversing the same FOV in the same direction at uniform 90-second intervals is geometrically consistent with (a) a fixed-period orbital pass observed against a static platform azimuth, (b) a regularly-spaced formation of targets crossing the sensor cone in sequence, or (c) a single target on a closed loop pattern returning past the sensor every 90 seconds. AARO commits to none of these.

**RU:** Три события **равномерно разнесены примерно на 90 секунд** (01:24 → 02:54 → 04:24 = 90 с + 90 с), с **идентичным направлением траверса «низ → верх»**. Это наиболее повторяющийся транзитный паттерн в сентябрьском 2020 подкластере и, возможно, один из наиболее структурно подозрительных в составе R02 PR: популяция целей, пересекающая одно и то же поле зрения в одном направлении с регулярным 90-секундным интервалом, геометрически согласуется с (а) орбитальным проходом фиксированного периода, наблюдаемым против статической азимутальной установки платформы, (б) регулярно разнесённой формацией целей, последовательно пересекающих конус сенсора, или (в) одной целью на замкнутой траектории, возвращающейся мимо сенсора каждые 90 секунд. AARO не обязывается ни к одному из этих вариантов.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Earliest record in the September 2020 sub-cluster / Самая ранняя запись сентябрьского 2020 подкластера:** PR087 (05 Sept) precedes PR085 (16 Sept) by 11 days and PR084 (17 Sept) by 12 days, making it the temporal anchor of the September 2020 CENTCOM trio. Combined with the previous batch's October 2020 records (PR079–PR083, dated 06–20 October 2020) and adjacent PR088/PR089 (31 AUG 2020), PR087 sits inside a **~5–7 week sustained UAP activity pattern (29 Aug → 20 Oct 2020) confined to CENTCOM AOR** — among the longest unambiguously temporally clustered patterns in the R02 PR set.
- **90-second periodicity is the most structurally distinctive feature in the September 2020 sub-cluster:** the uniform 90-s inter-event spacing across three transits is statistically unusual enough to warrant dedicated investigation. Three competing mechanisms — orbital-pass / sequential-formation / single-target-loop — each have testable consequences (ephemeris match / inter-target angular consistency / kinematic-loop closure) that frame-by-frame analysis of the raw file could discriminate.
- **Sensor-class inconsistency within the September 2020 sub-cluster:** PR084 and PR085 are described as "full-motion video camera"; PR087 (immediately adjacent in DVIDS ID and in incident date) is described as "infrared sensor." Either the same uploader curated captures from multiple platform classes during the same week (operationally plausible — mixed manned/unmanned ISR orbits in CENTCOM 2020), or AARO's description templates are not strictly synchronised across the cluster. Cross-check against PR079–PR083 sensor descriptions would inform.
- **Uniform direction (bottom→top) across all three events:** the consistent traversal direction is as analytically important as the 90-second periodicity. A bottom-to-top FOV crossing under typical aerial-ISR geometry (sensor pointing forward-down or forward-level) is consistent with targets passing **away from** the platform / **rising** through the platform's altitude band, or with **lateral passes** from the platform's right-rear quarter to right-front quarter (depending on roll/pitch). Geometric ambiguity is high without platform attitude.
- **Same-month upload preserves chain-of-custody:** the September 2020 upload (incident September 2020) gives PR087 the same tight CoC as PR084 and PR085, distinguishing the trio from PR086 (Dec 2019 incident / Sept 2020 upload, 9-month lag) and from many R02 PR records uploaded much later than the captured incident.
- **Boilerplate / disclaimer:** standard R02 51-records boilerplate header (March 6, 2026 House Representatives request, AARO collection from classified network, "many of these materials lack a substantiated chain-of-custody") and AARO non-analysis disclaimer both present.
