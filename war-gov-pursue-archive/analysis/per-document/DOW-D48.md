## Summary / Резюме

This is **not** a UAP mission report. It is a launch-safety-engineering risk-analysis monograph delivered by **Research Triangle Institute** and **ACTA Inc.** under USAF contract F04703-91-C-0112 to the AFSPC safety offices at the 45th Space Wing (Patrick AFB, Eastern Range) and the 30th Space Wing (Vandenberg AFB, Western Range), September 1996. Its subject is the modelling of *Mode-5 failure responses* — those unlikely launch-vehicle malfunctions that cause the booster to deviate from the flight line, sometimes substantially uprange or far crossrange, rather than impact near the pad.

The technical core of the report develops impact-density functions for Mode-5 failures inside RTI's **DAMP** ("facility DAMage and Personnel injury") risk-analysis program, fits two shaping constants (A, B) by trial-and-error against simulated malfunction-turn impacts for Atlas IIAS, Delta-GEM, Titan IV and Lockheed LLV-1, and derives empirical absolute and relative failure probabilities for Atlas, Delta and Titan from the historical record (532, 232, 337 flights respectively; plus 85 Eastern-Range Thor flights — 1,186 flights total).

The reason the document is in PURSUE Release 01 sits in **Appendix D**: a 180-page line-by-line **launch-and-failure narrative history** of every Atlas, Delta, Titan, and Thor flight from program start through August 1996, with each entry annotated for vehicle configuration, success/failure, flight phase of anomaly, and Mode classification. That appendix is one of the most concentrated unclassified failure-narrative datasets in the open literature on US space-launch anomalies — exactly the kind of baseline a UAP-screener would want when triaging *"is this a misidentified booster failure?"* against modern reports. Its inclusion in the war.gov UAP release is a **quiet contextualisation move**: the AARO / USCENTCOM compiler is making the long historical record of *prosaic* launch anomalies available alongside the modern UAP MISREPs, presumably so analysts can discriminate true anomaly from rocket-stage tumble, propellant chunk, or post-destruct debris.

The document carries no AARO disposition, no observer narrative, no UAP fields, and no redaction at all — it predates AARO by 26 years and was already unclassified when produced (only its distribution was restricted).

Предмет — моделирование режима отказа Mode-5 (отклонение ракеты-носителя от траектории полёта вверх по трассе или в сторону). Технический стержень — функция плотности точек падения для программы DAMP, с эмпирическими параметрами по 1186 полётам Atlas/Delta/Titan/Thor с начала программ по август 1996 г.

Документ включён в PURSUE Release 01 **в контекстуализирующих целях**: Приложение D содержит построчную историю отказов всех этих пусков — именно такой базовый материал необходим аналитику НЛО для определения: «является ли это неопознанной ступенью ракеты?» Никаких полей НЛО, никаких нарративов инцидентов, никаких редакций — документ уже был несекретным при создании (только с ограниченным распространением).
