## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute 35-second audio excerpt from Apollo 17 covering the in-flight crew commentary on small lights observed outside the spacecraft during transit to the Moon. Per AARO's description, Commander Gene Cernan, LMP Harrison Schmitt, and CMP Ronald Evans describe bright "particles" or "fragments" as being "jagged," "angular," and drifting near the Apollo spacecraft and the separated Saturn S-IVB stage. The crew themselves speculate in real time that paint chips or ice chips are the likely source; the particles are described as "twinkling" and moving away from the S-IVB.

**RU:** Аудиовыборка 4 минуты 35 секунд из Apollo 17, охватывающая полётный комментарий экипажа о небольших огнях, наблюдавшихся снаружи аппарата в ходе перелёта к Луне. По описанию AARO командир Gene Cernan, пилот лунного модуля Harrison Schmitt и пилот командного модуля Ronald Evans описывают яркие "частицы" или "фрагменты" как "зазубренные", "угловатые" и дрейфующие вблизи Apollo и отделённой ступени Saturn S-IVB. Сам экипаж в реальном времени предполагает, что вероятный источник — отколовшиеся частицы краски или льда; частицы описаны как "мерцающие" и удаляющиеся от S-IVB.

D009 is the audio companion to R01's `NASA-D2` (Apollo 17 voice transcript Block 1), which already established the S-IVB-separation particle-field exchange as closed in real time as routine launch-vehicle peeling / paint shedding. The AARO description summarises that the same closure logic is on the audio: the crew **itself** offers paint/ice attribution and notes the directional vector (away from the S-IVB), which makes this an unusually self-resolving exchange relative to the unresolved Gemini 7 bogey of `NASA-GEMINI-AUDIO` / `NASA-D3`. The Dec 7, 1972 date is also Apollo 17's launch day; the translunar phase aligns with the canonical S-IVB jettison and transposition-docking sequence (TLI + ~4 h), consistent with the timing implied by AARO's "transit to the moon" framing.

Описание AARO резюмирует, что та же логика закрытия — на аудио: **сам** экипаж предлагает атрибуцию краска/лёд и отмечает векторное направление (от S-IVB), что делает этот обмен необычно самозакрывающимся относительно нерешённого bogey Gemini 7 в `NASA-GEMINI-AUDIO` / `NASA-D3`. ## Notes / Замечания

- **Mission significance:** Apollo 17 is the **final** crewed Moon landing in human history and the only Apollo flight with a professional scientist (geologist Schmitt) on the lunar surface. The same mission produced the active-investigation `NASA-VM6` triangular-formation image; D009 is therefore tied to the most-investigated NASA-VM case in the corpus by mission identity.
- **NASA audio format:** Apollo air-to-ground audio is publicly available via NASA Archives and the Apollo Flight Journal; the AARO release-context for D009 is that AARO surfaces this specific excerpt as UAP-relevant, even though the in-exchange resolution (paint/ice) is itself non-anomalous.
- **Crew biographical context:** Cernan, Evans, and Schmitt are the canonical Apollo 17 crew; Schmitt's `NASA-D6` post-flight light-flash debrief is the second-most-investigated NASA-D entry. D009 captures the **same crew** at the **opposite end** of the mission narrative (outbound translunar coast vs. post-flight debrief).

The R02 audio family is intentionally chronology-shuffled relative to the R01 D1–D7 PDF family.
