## Summary / Резюме

A 33 SOS / 27 SOW special-mission ISR sortie launched from Larisa AB (LGLR, Greece) at 0109Z on 25 January 2024 in support of a tasking masked under `(b)(1)1.4a`. The sortie was a 20h40m round-trip back to LGLR, with on-station 0635Z–1504Z (8h29m) over the fragged target area at MGRS `36RXV19…[masked]`.

While **in transit** at **250509Z** — 4 hours after takeoff and 1.5 hours before reaching the on-station ISR point — the asset observed **1× UAP** at MGRS `35SQT44…[masked]` (Eastern Mediterranean / Aegean), trajectory westward, at kinetic altitude **FL200**. The friendly aircraft was at FL250, trajectory 162°, speed 176 KTS. The UAP was **observed for ~2 minutes**, lost at 0511Z, with the description **"ROUND DIAMOND SHAPE WITH STRAIGHT, NON MANUEVERABLE 'TAIL'"** — and the further explicit **"THE UAP APPEARED DIAMOND IN SHAPE WITH A NON-MANUEVERING PROBE AT THE BOTTOM. THE UAP ONLY APPEARED ON THE SWIR CAMERA."** Initial speed at first observation: **approximately 434 knots**. UAP Signatures: **"SWIR WHT"** (SWIR-channel only, polarity white-hot).

UAP Anomalous Characteristics / Behaviors: **"MAINTAINED STEADY FLIGHT PATH, INCREASED AND DECREASED ALTITUDE PROFILE, DID NOT CHANGE TRAJECTORY"**. UAP Physical State: Solid. UAP Under Intelligent Control: UNK. UAP Advanced Capabilities: NO. Observer Assessment: Benign. Observer was a Senior Airman (E-4) from 27 SOW. UAP Event Serial Number: **`250509ZJAN2024-CENTCOM 001`** — the only PURSUE D-series record carrying a fully-formed CENTCOM UAP serial-number suffix `001`.

After the brief 2-minute encounter, the asset continued to the SP at 0635Z, conducted POL (pattern-of-life) observation through 1353Z (no EEI activity), then 1353Z–1500Z conducted a 1× stop follow on 1× ADM (adult male) in a silver SUV, and at 1400Z observed a large white box being transferred from a black SUV to a white van. Returned to base 1504Z, landed LGLR 2149Z. Total mission 20h40m, 8h29m FMV, 8h27m SIGINT, 1× FMV tasking and 1× SIGINT tasking prosecuted.

В **250509Z**, на пути к заданной точке, **1× НЛО** замечен по MGRS `35SQT44...` (Восточное Средиземноморье / Эгейское море) на высоте **FL200** с западной траекторией. Дружественный ЛА — FL250, курс 162°, 176 KTS. НЛО наблюдался **~2 минуты**, утерян в 0511Z. Описание: **«ROUND DIAMOND SHAPE WITH STRAIGHT, NON MANUEVERABLE 'TAIL'»** и **«THE UAP ONLY APPEARED ON THE SWIR CAMERA»** (обнаружен только каналом коротковолнового ИК). ## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. **Is D25 the true companion to PR28?** AARO's PR28 metadata says the paired MISREP is D7, describing a "diamond-shape, 434 knots, SWIR-only" UAP. PURSUE's D7 is a different document (Arabian Gulf 2020 balloon-like object). D25 is the *only* CENTCOM record matching all three diagnostic features (diamond, 434 KTS, SWIR-only). Either AARO's metadata mislabels the MISREP id, or there is an unreleased D7 version whose content matches PR28's description.

   **RU:** Действительно ли D25 — истинный компаньон PR28? AARO указывает D7, но D7 — это другой документ (Арабский залив 2020, баллонообразный объект). D25 — единственная запись CENTCOM, совпадающая по всем трём признакам (ромб, 434 уз., только SWIR). Скорее всего, это ошибочная метка в метаданных AARO PR28.

2. SWIR-only detectability is a notable instrumentation fingerprint — the object was below the noise threshold of both visible-light EO and longwave IR, but visible in shortwave IR (~1.0–1.7 μm). What surface or material composition would explain the channel-selective detectability?

   **RU:** Обнаружение только в канале SWIR (1,0–1,7 мкм) — значимый инструментальный отпечаток: объект невидим в видимом диапазоне и MWIR/LWIR. Какой состав поверхности или материала объясняет избирательную видимость?

3. The "non-manuevering probe at the bottom" feature recurs in D27 and PR29 ("vertical unwavering cylindrical pole/bar attached on the bottom" / "inverted teardrop with vertically linear trailing mass"). D27's operator hypothesises this could be **a reflection from the object in the water**. If the same hypothesis applies to D25, the object would have to be near the surface — but D25's kinetic altitude is FL200 (20,000 ft MSL), inconsistent with sea-surface reflection. The morphology may therefore be a **physical feature** of the object.

   **RU:** Признак «неподвижный зонд внизу» повторяется в D27 и PR29. Оператор D27 допускает отражение от поверхности воды, но высота D25 — FL200 (6100 м), что исключает такую интерпретацию. Возможно, это физическая особенность объекта.

4. UAP Event Serial Number `250509ZJAN2024-CENTCOM 001` carries the suffix `001`. D28 explicitly notes the CCMD-assigned numerical sequence is unknown at the time of filing. Was D25 the inaugural CCMD-numbered UAP submission for CENTCOM in calendar 2024?

   **RU:** Серийный номер с суффиксом `001` предполагает, что это первая нумерованная UAP-заявка CENTCOM в 2024 г. D28 явно указывает, что такая последовательность была неизвестна на момент подачи. Был ли D25 самой первой?

5. The UAP encounter happened **in transit**, before the asset reached the fragged SP. The pattern matches D33 (also "while transiting") and D27 ("DURING RTB"). Worth flagging that **CENTCOM AFSOC ISR sorties produce most of their UAP encounters in transit phases, not in target areas**.

   **RU:** Встреча произошла **на транзите**, до достижения заданной точки. То же характерно для D33 (транзит) и D27 (RTB). Большинство встреч с НЛО в вылетах AFSOC ISR CENTCOM фиксируется в транзитных фазах, а не в целевых районах.
