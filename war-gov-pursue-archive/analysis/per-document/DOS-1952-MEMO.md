## Summary / Резюме

The metadata states the document discusses:

- "historical records of UFOs"
- "U.S. Air Force opinions on UFOs"

The memo is filed under State Department decimal classification **711.5612** — the 711-series is "Foreign Relations / United States — Specific Foreign Countries" and 711.56 covers air-force / military-aviation relations; the 711.5612 sub-decimal is the air-attache / aerial-incident series. This filing location is consistent with a State Department policy memo prepared in response to questions or requests for guidance from foreign governments or U.S. embassies, regarding how to characterize the surge in domestic UFO reports.
