## Summary / Резюме

A 3-minute-57-second infrared sensor clip from a U.S. military platform operating in USEUCOM AOR in August 2022, with uploader-attributed phenomenology "Spherical UAP Erratic movement". AARO's structural description splits the clip into two segments: 00:00–00:45 features an area of contrast in the center of the frame with sensor pan-tracking; 00:46–03:57 (after an apparent cut) re-focuses on an area of contrast in the center as the sensor pans to track it while the target intermittently passes in and out of frame. Critically, AARO explicitly warns that the digital alterations **"significantly influence the area of contrast's apparent performance characteristics"** — the single most explicit "uploader edits distort the kinematics" disclaimer in any R02 PR record in this batch.

Структурное описание AARO делит клип на два сегмента: 00:00–00:45 содержит область контраста в центре кадра с панорамированием сенсора; 00:46–03:57 (после явного стыка) повторно фокусируется на области контраста в центре, в то время как сенсор панорамирует для слежения, а цель периодически выходит из кадра и возвращается.
