## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute-50-second dual-modality clip (FMV camera + IR sensor) from a U.S. military platform in CENTCOM AOR, dated **2021-04-12** per the uploader title "Spherical UAP [CALLSIGN] 2021/04/12 obj 2". AARO assesses 2021 as the capture year but does not endorse the calendar date. The file was uploaded to a classified network in June 2024 — a three-year provenance gap. The video description gives three stages: 00:00–00:26 an area of contrast transits the FOV right-to-left with pan-and-zoom track; 00:27–02:04 the area of contrast re-enters and is tracked panning left and up; 02:05–04:50 the sensor "refocuses on the original subject matter of the video" — implying the area of contrast was a secondary subject and the sensor returned to the platform's primary task.

**RU:** Дуо-режимный клип (FMV-камера + ИК-сенсор) продолжительностью 4 минуты 50 секунд с военной платформы США в зоне ответственности CENTCOM, датированный **2021-04-12** по заголовку загрузчика «Spherical UAP [CALLSIGN] 2021/04/12 obj 2». AARO оценивает 2021 как год съёмки, но не подтверждает календарную дату. Файл загружен в засекреченную сеть в июне 2024 — трёхлетний разрыв в провенансе. Описание видео даёт три этапа: 00:00–00:26 область контраста транзитом проходит поле зрения справа налево с панорамированием и зумом; 00:27–02:04 область контраста снова входит в поле зрения и отслеживается панорамированием влево и вверх; 02:05–04:50 сенсор «перефокусируется на исходный предмет видеосъёмки» — что подразумевает, что область контраста была вторичным предметом, а сенсор вернулся к основной задаче платформы.

## Notes / Замечания

- **2021/04/12 single-encounter cluster — four segmented clips, not one merged video:** AARO chose to release four DVIDS assets (PR060 "obj 2" + PR061 "vid 0" + PR062 "vid 1" + PR063 "vid 2") for what is almost certainly a single encounter with a single platform on a single date. This is the **only** four-clip single-encounter cluster in the R02 PR050+ block and is unprecedented in Release 01 (where most PR records are 1:1 with their encounter). The segmentation likely reflects the source-side reality: an operator/analyst on the classified network saved discrete clips during the encounter rather than recording a continuous file, and AARO preserved that segmentation rather than re-stitching.
- **Uploader's "spherical" vs AARO's "area of contrast":** the uploader-side morphological commitment ("Spherical UAP") was preserved by AARO in the title, but AARO's own video description sticks to the neutral "area of contrast". This is consistent with the PURSUE corpus pattern of letting uploader-side semantics through in titles while keeping analytical language neutral in descriptions.
- **"obj 2" naming grammar suggests ≥2 objects:** the filename schema in this cluster distinguishes object identity ("obj N") from segment position ("vid N"). PR060 is the only "obj"-coded clip; PR061–PR063 are "vid"-coded sequential segments. The most parsimonious reading is that the encounter involved at least two tracked objects, and PR060 documents the **second** one while PR061–PR063 are sequential segments tracking the **first** (or another) object.
- **Provenance gap / Разрыв в провенансе:** 2021 capture → June 2024 classified-network upload (three-year gap). Same boilerplate caveat as PR059.
- **AARO modality call: dual-modality (FMV + IR)** — informative; distinguishes the 2021/04/12 platform from the 2020 "NAG" platform (PR059, IR-only) and from the "color + IR" sibling PR062 (color FMV — possibly daytime EO band). Mode mix across the cluster suggests at least two sensor heads or a multi-mode turret.
