## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute infrared sensor clip from a U.S. military platform in USCENTCOM AOR. The uploader title commits to "3 fast moving UAP's" but AARO's video-description timeline explicitly resolves only **two** simultaneous areas of contrast (the second enters frame at 01:02 while the first is still tracked; both remain in frame ~15 s). The third "fast-moving" UAP claimed by the uploader is not separately described by AARO. This is the **largest disagreement between uploader title and AARO timeline in the October-2020 cluster**.

**RU:** Четырёхминутный клип инфракрасного сенсора с военной платформы США в зоне ответственности USCENTCOM. Заголовок загрузчика обязывается «3 быстро движущихся НЛО», однако таймлайн видео-описания AARO явно фиксирует только **две** одновременные области контраста (вторая входит в кадр в 01:02, пока первая ещё отслеживается; обе остаются в кадре ~15 с). Третий «быстро движущийся» НЛО, заявленный загрузчиком, AARO отдельно не описывает. Это **самое крупное расхождение между заголовком загрузчика и таймлайном AARO в кластере октября 2020**.

PR079 is the **highest-numbered** record in the October-2020 cluster (DVIDS 1007816 — terminal asset of the 1007706–1007816 R02 PR-range) and **chronologically the latest** event in the cluster. Its 4-minute runtime makes it the longest clip in the five-record window — three times the duration of the shortest (PR083 is also four-minute-class, others are five-minute-class but with most of the runtime "no content"). The structural pattern AARO describes (track → zoom → track → second target enters → first exits → sensor-cycle noise → faint single track → noise) is **operator working hard on a multi-target scene**, not the single-pass transit pattern dominant in PR081/PR082.

**RU:** PR079 — **запись с самым высоким номером** в кластере октября 2020 (DVIDS 1007816 — терминальный актив диапазона PR R02 1007706–1007816) и **хронологически самое позднее** событие в кластере. Структурный паттерн, описываемый AARO (трек → зум → трек → вторая цель входит → первая выходит → шум смены режимов → одиночный слабый трек → шум), — это **оператор, активно работающий по многоцелевой сцене**, а не паттерн одиночного прохода, доминирующий в PR081/PR082.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Cluster terminus / Терминус кластера:** PR079 is the chronologically latest event (29 Oct 2020) and the highest DVIDS-numbered record (1007816) in the October-2020 cluster — it closes both the 22-day calendar window and the R02 PR DVIDS range. The terminal-position concurrence may be coincidence, but it is worth flagging as a possible AARO release-ordering signal.
- **Multi-target framing without multi-target evidence:** the uploader-asserted "3" vs AARO-resolved "2" gap is the largest title-vs-timeline arithmetic mismatch in the October-2020 cluster. This matters because the cluster's only explicit multi-object framing comes from PR079 — the other four records are single-track or single-transit framings.
- **75+ seconds of "no content" in a 240-s clip:** ~31% null payload is high for a "Mission observes" framing. Either AARO trimmed nothing from a longer master, or the original operator left the recorder running across slew/zoom transitions where nothing was visible. Track this against the AARO-envelope-vs-file-length pattern documented across the R02 PR050–PR099 cluster.
- **Lexical commitment:** uploader writes "UAP's" (plural, possessive-apostrophe variant); AARO holds to "area(s) of contrast". This is the standard R02 pattern — AARO never adopts uploader vocabulary, even where the title is highly committed.
- **Boilerplate / Стандартный текст:** R02 51-records boilerplate paragraph present (March 6, 2026 House Representatives request, AARO collection from classified network, CoC caveat).
