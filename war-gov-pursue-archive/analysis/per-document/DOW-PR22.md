A 14-second split-screen sensor clip from a U.S. military platform showing infrared (left) and electro-optical (right) feeds simultaneously. Per AARO's metadata, "An accompanying mission report, DoW-UAP-D16, described the UAP as 'moving from north to south.'" Video description: at 00:05, "the video depicts an object moving from right to left across the top right quarter of the sensor field-of-view."

По метаданным AARO: "Сопроводительный доклад о миссии, DoW-UAP-D16, описал НЛО как 'движущийся с севера на юг'." PR22 is one of only two PR clips in the release that provide simultaneous IR + EO views of the same encounter (the other is PR28's IR + EO + SWIR composite for ten seconds before SWIR-only). The split-feed format is informationally richer than single-modality clips: it lets an analyst compare a thermal signature to a visible signature for the same target in the same instant. Notably, AARO uses the word "object" in PR22's video description rather than the standard "area of contrast" — a small but unusual lexical commitment.
