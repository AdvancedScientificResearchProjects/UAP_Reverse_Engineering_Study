## Summary / Резюме

A 1-minute-19-second single-modality infrared sensor clip that AARO assesses is "likely derived from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform operating within the United States Central Command area of responsibility in 2018." The uploader-assigned title "HH11 03 July 2018 UAPs" implies a capture date of **3 July 2018** and tags the source with the token "HH11" (plausibly a helicopter callsign, given the "HH" prefix used by HH-60 Pave Hawk and related CSAR/utility rotary-wing platforms). The file was uploaded to a classified network in July 2020, a two-year gap after the implied capture date. The video description gives a four-stage timeline: two areas of contrast enter at 00:13 from lower-right and exit the bottom; the sensor pans 00:13–00:29 to centre them; zoom-in at 00:30–00:47 resolves one feature into **three distinct areas of contrast in a generally straight line**; at 00:47–00:58 the rightmost target separates from a stable centre-left pair; at 00:59–01:19 additional areas of contrast enter from the top of the screen.

Присвоенный загрузчиком заголовок «HH11 03 July 2018 UAPs» подразумевает дату съёмки **3 июля 2018** и помечает источник токеном «HH11» (предположительно вертолётный позывной, учитывая префикс «HH», используемый HH-60 Pave Hawk и родственными платформами вращающегося крыла CSAR/утилитарного типа). PR096 is **the earliest R02 PR-record by encounter year (2018)** — the only 2018-dated record in the entire PURSUE PR corpus. It sits in a temporal gap of R01: R01's CENTCOM PR-records cluster around 2013 (`PR38`), 2016 (`D55`), and 2020–2022, with no 2018 representation. PR096 closes that gap. The morphology is analytically interesting: the **three-collinear contrast features** at the higher zoom level (00:30–00:47) and the **stable pair + diverging straggler** evolution at 00:47–00:58 are geometrically consistent with either (a) a multi-object formation undergoing dispersion, or (b) sensor-resolution artifacts where a single source resolves into a triplet at higher magnification (e.g. a closely-spaced flare drop, a multi-glint object, or a low-altitude bird formation seen against a hot land background). The "HH11" uploader tag is the analytically interesting unknown, parallel to PR059's unredacted "NAG" token: AARO did not transcribe or interpret it.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Temporal anchor — only 2018 PR in the corpus / Временной якорь — единственный PR 2018 в корпусе:** PR096 fills the 2017–2019 gap in the CENTCOM PR-record timeline. Before R02 the CENTCOM PR-cluster jumped from `PR38` (2013) and `D55` (2016) directly to 2019+ records. PR096 is now the sole 2018 representative.
- **HH-prefix rotary-wing platform inference / Вывод о платформе вращающегося крыла по префиксу HH:** the "HH11" tag carries the HH- prefix family signature (HH-60 Pave Hawk, HH-65 Dolphin) more typical of CSAR/utility helicopter callsigns than fixed-wing fighter/ISR designators. If accurate, this would be the first identifiably-rotary-wing-platform PR in the corpus (most other CENTCOM PRs are fixed-wing ISR / FMV turrets).
- **R02 PR050–PR099 terminal block / Завершающий блок R02 PR050–PR099:** PR096 opens the final four-record block of the R02 PR cluster (PR096, PR097, PR098, PR099). PR097 and PR099 share a single 25 SEP 2019 date with two ~4-hour-separated Zulu times; PR098 is the multi-target Persian Gulf "formation?" record. Together with PR096 these four cards complete per-document coverage of the R02 PR050–PR099 50-record cluster.
- **Uploader-token preservation pattern / Шаблон сохранения пользовательского токена:** PR096 ("HH11") and PR059 ("NAG") are the two known R02 PR records where AARO published the uploader's title token without redaction. Either a redaction inconsistency or a deliberate signal that the tokens were judged non-sensitive (callsigns sufficiently generic, units sufficiently inferable, dates already declassified).
- **Boilerplate / Стандартный текст:** the standard R02 House-of-Representatives provenance paragraph and non-analysis disclaimer are both present.
