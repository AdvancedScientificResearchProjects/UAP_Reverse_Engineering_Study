## Summary / Резюме

A US Navy MH-60R Seahawk from **HSM-73** (Helicopter Maritime Strike Squadron
73 "Battle Cats"), side number **705**, BUNO **168122**, observed **three (3)
possible unidentified small air contacts** while conducting Routine Operations
(SSC — Surface Surveillance Coordination) over the North Arabian Sea at
00:04:30Z on 24 August 2020 (night-time).

Per the operator's narrative paragraph:

> **Observed 3x possible unidentified small air contact while conducting
> routine operations in the North Arabian Sea. Negative ES, radar track, and
> IFF track. Distance to contact was unknown. Speed of contact was unknown.
> Precise course of contact was unknown, but appeared to be on a westerly
> heading. No interaction took place between [redacted 1.4a] and the unknown
> air contacts. Initially observed 1x unknown air contact and tracked it
> before losing sight as it went behind a cloud. When contact on the unknown
> air contact was regained, 2x additional unknown air contacts were seen due
> east of the location of the initial contact. All 3x unknown air contacts
> appeared to maintain their relative course, speed, and altitude.**

The structured checkbox fields capture the operator's classification choices:
**Round**, **Wings/Airframe**, **Translucent** (ticked); Square, Balloon-shaped,
Other Shape, Apparent Propulsion, Markings, Moving Parts, Metallic, Opaque,
Reflective (not ticked). Number of contacts in "group" = **3**. EA Indications:
none ticked. Wind direction at contact altitude **270°**, wind speed **5 KTS**.

The "Negative ES, radar track, and IFF track" line is operationally important:
contacts were detected **visually only**, with **no electronic-support
(ES/EW) signal, no radar return, and no IFF interrogation response** —
consistent with small low-RCS optical-only objects, but inconsistent with
conventional manned aircraft, which would normally squawk IFF Mode-3 or present
a clear radar return for the MH-60R's APS-153 radar.

The phenomenological pattern — **single contact lost behind cloud; reacquired
plus two additional contacts due east; all three then maintaining relative
course / speed / altitude in formation, on a westerly heading** — is unusual
and is the **canonical "unknown small drone group"** signature that the
AARO / SPEAR Range Fouler program was designed to capture.

**RU:** Вертолёт MH-60R Seahawk из состава HSM-73 («Battle Cats»), бортовой номер 705, BUNO 168122, наблюдал **три (3) возможных неопознанных малых воздушных контакта** во время рутинной операции SSC (Surface Surveillance Coordination) над Северным Аравийским морем в 00:04:30Z 24 августа 2020 г. (ночное время). «Отрицательный результат по ES, радарному трeku и IFF» — контакты обнаружены **только визуально** без электронного, радарного и транспондерного отклика. Паттерн: 1 контакт → потеря за облаком → повторное обнаружение + 2 новых контакта к востоку → все три поддерживают относительный курс/скорость/высоту в строю на западном курсе. Чекбоксы: Round + Wings/Airframe + Translucent (отмечены); Metallic, Apparent Propulsion и другие — нет. D56 — **единственная запись Range Fouler вертолёта ВМС США (MH-60R / HSM-73)** в PURSUE Release 01.

## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. **Which carrier or surface combatant was HSM-73 embarked on?** August 2020 5th Fleet deployments included USS Nimitz CSG (CVW-17 — HSM-73's normal wing) and USS Bataan / USS Bonhomme Richard ESG. HSM-73's BUNO 168122 and the O-3 rank do not narrow this further. **RU:** На каком корабле базировался HSM-73? USS Nimitz CSG (CVW-17, штатное крыло HSM-73) наиболее вероятен.

2. **What were the three contacts?** Checkboxes (Round + Wings/Airframe + **Translucent**) are an unusual combination — "Translucent" is not commonly ticked in the Range-Fouler corpus. Candidate explanations: (a) small consumer/commercial drones (translucent drones are non-standard), (b) balloons (but "Wings/Airframe" excludes pure balloons), (c) anomalous optical phenomena, (d) birds (translucent wings of large wading birds), (e) small RCS UAVs with radar-absorbing structure. **RU:** Кандидаты: потребительские БПЛА (нестандартная прозрачность), аэростаты (исключает Wings/Airframe), птицы (прозрачные крылья), БПЛА с РПМ-покрытием. Сочетание Round + Wings/Airframe + Translucent уникально в корпусе Range Fouler.

3. **What were MH-60R's altitude and the contacts' altitude?** Both masked (`1.4a`). MH-60R typical SSC orbit is ~200–500 ft AGL; contacts at similar low altitude over open ocean could be visible against the moonlit sky. **RU:** Обе высоты засекречены; типичная орбита SSC MH-60R — 200–500 ft AGL.

4. **Why the "Translucent" classification?** First PURSUE Range Fouler with the Translucent box ticked (D38, D44, D57 left it blank; D58 has Metallic + Opaque + Reflective). **RU:** Первый Range Fouler в PURSUE с отмеченным «Translucent» (прозрачный/просвечивающий). Оперативно значимая категория.

5. **Was there any follow-up?** "No interaction took place" closes the encounter; unknown contacts continued on their westerly heading. No recovery, no escalation, no AARO disposition. **RU:** «Взаимодействия не было»; контакты продолжили западный курс. Дальнейших действий нет.

6. **Display tape upload?** The form notes that display tapes should be uploaded as .wmv to the repository. Did HSM-73 upload an FLIR / video clip? If yes, it is not in PURSUE Release 01. **RU:** Форма предписывает загрузку видео (display tape .wmv). Если вертолёт записал контакты, видео в PURSUE Release 01 отсутствует.

- **"Negative ES, radar track, and IFF track" — strong negative-correlation signature:** Three independent detection modalities returned null on visually-confirmed contacts. This is the **most operationally meaningful observation in the document**: constrains the contacts' profile to a narrow set of mundane explanations (small low-RCS objects with no transponder) plus the standard UAP-anomaly hypothesis space. **RU:** Три независимые модальности обнаружения (ES/ЭМ, радар, IFF) дали нулевой результат при визуальном подтверждении — наиболее оперативно значимый факт документа.
