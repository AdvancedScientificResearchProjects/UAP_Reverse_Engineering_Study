## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute-49-second dual-modality clip from a U.S. military platform in CENTCOM AOR, dated **2021-04-12** per the uploader title "Spherical UAP [CALLSIGN] 2021/04/12 vid 1". This is the only clip in the four-record 2021/04/12 cluster that AARO assesses as **"color and infrared sensor"** rather than "full-motion video camera and infrared sensor" — implying explicit visible-band color imagery, not just grayscale FMV. The file was uploaded to a classified network in **May 2024** — one month before the rest of the cluster (June 2024), suggesting a separate ingest event. The video description is unusually compact: a single line covering 00:00–04:49 — "The sensor pans and cycles zoom levels to track an area of contrast, keeping it generally within the center of the sensor field-of-view."

**RU:** Дуо-режимный клип продолжительностью 4 минуты 49 секунд с военной платформы США в зоне ответственности CENTCOM, датированный **2021-04-12** по заголовку загрузчика «Spherical UAP [CALLSIGN] 2021/04/12 vid 1». Это единственный клип в четырёхзаписном кластере 2021/04/12, который AARO оценивает как **«цветной и инфракрасный сенсор»**, а не «камера FMV и инфракрасный сенсор» — что подразумевает явное цветное изображение в видимом диапазоне, а не только полутоновое FMV. Файл загружен в засекреченную сеть в **мае 2024** — за месяц до остальной части кластера (июнь 2024), что указывает на отдельное событие ингеста. Описание видео необычно компактно: одна строка, покрывающая 00:00–04:49 — «Сенсор панорамирует и циклирует уровни зума для отслеживания области контраста, удерживая её в центре поля зрения сенсора».

PR062 is the **most informationally compact** stub in the four-clip cluster — AARO documents a single sustained ~4:49 track with no narrative subdivision. Combined with the dual-modality call (color + IR) and the earlier upload date (May 2024 vs June 2024 for siblings), PR062 is **best-case footage** of the encounter: continuous track, central FOV, multi-band sensor coverage. This is consistent with the operator having time to switch the platform's color sensor on after the initial detection clip (PR061) and to refine the track before the object eventually became too indistinct to follow.

В сочетании с двумя режимами (цвет + ИК) и более ранней датой загрузки (май 2024 против июня 2024 для родственных) PR062 представляет **наилучшие кадры** встречи: непрерывный трек, центр поля зрения, многодиапазонное покрытие сенсора. ## Notes / Замечания

- **The "best-case" segment of the four-clip 2021/04/12 cluster:** PR062 combines (a) the longest sustained on-center track in the cluster, (b) the only "color" modality call, and (c) an earlier upload timestamp. These three facts jointly suggest PR062 carries the highest-information-density frames of the encounter — most worth a forensic frame-by-frame review.
- **"Color and infrared" vs "FMV and infrared" — sensor-class divergence within the cluster:** AARO's modality language varies across the four clips (PR060/PR061/PR063 = "FMV + IR"; PR062 = "color + IR"). This could reflect (a) a true sensor mode-switch within the encounter — the operator activated the visible-band color band for PR062's segment, then reverted — or (b) AARO labelling inconsistency for the same underlying multi-band sensor. Interpretation requires the actual mp4 inspection.
- **Out-of-sync upload timing:** PR062 ingested May 2024; PR060/PR061/PR063 ingested June 2024. The split timing implies the cluster was not uploaded as a single batch — likely two separate user actions, possibly two different users. AARO's boilerplate "uploaded to a classified network" does not name uploaders, but the timing divergence is a chain-of-custody signal worth recording.
- **Four-clip-from-single-encounter pattern:** see PR060 Notes for the cluster-level discussion. PR062 is the "vid 1" segment and the multi-band outlier within the cluster.
- **Provenance gap / Разрыв в провенансе:** 2021 capture → May 2024 classified-network upload (three-year gap). Same boilerplate caveat as the rest of the cluster.
