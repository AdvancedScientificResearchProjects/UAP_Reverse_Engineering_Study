## Summary / Резюме

USCENTCOM long-endurance ISR sortie (20:56 mission, 15.2 IMINT hours, 19.3
SIGINT hours) launched from **OKAS** (Ali Al Salem AB, Kuwait) at 152302Z on
15 September 2020, recovered to OKAS at 161958Z on 16 September. Operator unit
`482ATKS` / `432 AEW`; AN/DAS-4 turret + AH/GMESH avionics — same airframe
class as D61, almost certainly the same MQ-9 Reaper community. Mission supported
NAVCENT ISO IRIN/IRGCN vessel characterisation, UAS activity, and
pattern-of-life across the Arabian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, and Gulf of Oman.

This MISREP is **the densest single-sortie anomaly record in the 2020 Arabian
Gulf cluster**. On a single sortie, the aircraft logged:

- **3 GUARDCALLs from Iranian Air Defense** (0408Z, 0421Z, 1141Z — two DIRECTIVE, one PROFESSIONAL)
- **2 anomalous EMI lost-link events**, both classified **"Type of EMI: UNKNOWN"**, **"EMI Impact to System: Complete"**, **"Mission Changed: Flight Path Deviation"**, **"Mission Impact: MEDIUM"**:
  - **EMI 1**: 161248Z–161259Z, 11 minutes, at `39RXK0...`, FL180, 89 KIAS — *"EXPERIENCED LOST LINK TO POSSIBLE [1.4g], [1.4a]. REGAINED LINK AT 1259Z"* (JSIR ID330412)
  - **EMI 2**: 161414Z–161441:16Z, 27 minutes, at `39RVM9...`, FL180, 120 KIAS — *"EXPERIENCED LOST LINK TO POSSIBLE [1.4g] [1.4a]. REGAINED 1441Z"* (JSIR ID330414)
- **1 UAP observation** at 161732Z, ~3 hours after the second EMI, at `39RVM5...`, FL180 / 90 KIAS, method FMV

The UAP itself is logged with the absolute minimum narrative: **"AT 1732Z, [redacted 1.4a] OBSERVED A UAP IVO 39RVM5...[1.4a]"** — no shape, no count, no kinematics, no maneuverability, no signatures, no engagement. The ~6 km separation between the second EMI grid (`39RVM9`) and the UAP grid (`39RVM5`) is well within the orbit footprint — the EMI events and UAP could plausibly involve the same physical airspace volume, ~3 hours apart.

The MISREP **does not link the EMI to the UAP**. JSIR IDs are populated for
the EMI events only. But the timing, the same orbit, and the **"UNKNOWN" EMI
type with COMPLETE system impact** make this the strongest candidate in the
corpus for a *physically associated* EMI / UAP pairing within a single sortie.

**RU:** Длительный ISR-вылет 482 ATKS / 432 AEW (20:56, 15,2 IMINT, 19,3 SIGINT), 15–16 сентября 2020 г. — **наиболее насыщенная аномалиями однодневная запись** в кластере Арабского залива 2020 г. За один вылет: 3 иранских «гард-вызова»; 2 аномальных события ЭМИ с полной потерей связи (11 мин и 27 мин), тип ЭМИ «UNKNOWN», воздействие «Complete», отклонение от маршрута; 1 наблюдение НЛО в 1732Z с минимальным нарративом (только «OBSERVED A UAP», без формы, числа, кинематики). ЭМИ-2 (39RVM9) и НЛО (39RVM5) разделены ~6 км и ~3 часами — возможная физическая ассоциация, не подтверждённая в MISREP. D62 и D58 — **единственные записи с совместным появлением ЭМИ и кинетического НЛО** в современном кластере DoW.

## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. **Are the two EMI events and the UAP physically associated?** Indicators *for*: same orbit, same shift, all three within 5h, EMI 2 ≈6 km from UAP, both EMI typed UNKNOWN with COMPLETE impact. Indicators *against*: MISREP does not link them; UAP follows EMI 2 by ~3 hours (not co-temporal); Iranian coastal littoral is a high-EW environment with multiple non-anomalous lost-link causes. **RU:** За ассоциацию: одна орбита, 5 часов, 6 км, тип ЭМИ UNKNOWN. Против: MISREP не связывает, 3-часовой разрыв, высокая плотность ЭМИ в Ормузском проливе.

2. **What is the affected system?** "Affected System" masked under `1.4g`. For MQ-9: C2 datalink (Ku-band SATCOM via DGS-1) or LOS datalink (CDL). COMPLETE impact + 11/27-minute durations + Flight Path Deviation indicate genuine link failure, not a transient glitch. **RU:** «Affected System» засекречено; для MQ-9 — Ku-band SATCOM (DGS-1) или LOS CDL. Полная потеря на 11/27 минут — реальный разрыв связи, не переходный сбой.

3. **The "POSSIBLE [1.4g] [1.4a]" suspect** in EMI gentexts suggests the operator suspected a *specific named source* (Iranian EW system or US-attributed jamming) — this hypothesis is held in the redacted fields. **RU:** Формулировка «POSSIBLE [1.4g] [1.4a]» указывает, что оператор подозревал конкретный источник ЭМИ (иранская система РЭА?), скрытый под `1.4g`.

4. **Why does the UAP entry omit all phenomenology?** Either the operator had no further detail, or details are in the FMV exploit by DGS-1 and were not transcribed into the MISREP. No PR-series video for this date has been identified in PURSUE Release 01. **RU:** Нарратив минимален: возможно, у оператора не было деталей, или они содержатся в FMV-эксплойте DGS-1, не включённом в MISREP.

5. **Cross-link to D58:** D58 (independent airframe, F-15E DCA, different week, different form) is the only other case where a known EW indication co-occurs with an unidentified kinetic contact. Together, D58 + D62 suggest a 2020-era USCENTCOM AOR EMI/UAP pattern that warrants its own thematic write-up. **RU:** D58 независим (другая платформа, другая неделя), но единственный другой случай в 2020 г. CENTCOM с ЭМИ + кинетический НЛО. Вместе D58+D62 формируют тематический паттерн.
