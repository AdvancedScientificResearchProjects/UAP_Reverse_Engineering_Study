## Summary / Резюме

This is **not** a MISREP and not an incident report — it is an **inter-agency
email exchange** between an OUSD(I&S) Information Disclosure Analyst and a
PAROC Intel Data Analysis Technician Team Lead at **12 AF / DET 3**, on the
question of **what classification level** two short UAP-event "tearlines" can
be released at.

The thread documents a routine declassification-confirmation step that AARO and
its supporting J2 / IO disclosure analysts must perform before any UAP narrative
can be published in war.gov-style releases: take the raw S//NF mission-report
text, ask the parent unit (the squadron that flew the asset, identity masked
under `1.4(a)`) to confirm what can be said in the open, and produce
**"//UNCLASSIFIED//"-bracketed tearlines** that strip out unit identifiers, ATO
numbers, AOR identifiers (where unit declines), and intelligence-source
methodology.

The two tearlines released here are remarkably brief — each a single sentence
naming the date, observation duration, time-Z, and the nulled-out altitude /
speed / interference fields:

> 1.4(a) on 10APR25 — //UNCLASSIFIED// US AIRCRAFT OBSERVED 1X POSS UAP FOR
> 12 SECONDS AT 2353Z, FLYING AT UNK ALTITUDE AND UNK SPEED, NO INTERFERENCE
> WAS NOTED. //UNCLASSIFIED//
>
> 1.4(a) on 11APR25 — //UNCLASSIFIED// US AIRCRAFT OBSERVED 1X POSS UAP FOR
> 23 SECONDS AT 0007Z, FLYING AT UNK ALTITUDE AND UNK SPEED, NO INTERFERENCE
> WAS NOTED. //UNCLASSIFIED//

The clarification thread also confirms that **adding the AOR designator
"INDOPACOM" is at the UNCLASSIFIED level** — that is, the Indo-Pacific
geographic attribution can be published, even though the specific country,
basing location, and platform identity remain at S//NF.

What the thread does *not* contain: any descriptive phenomenology (no shape, no
colour, no behaviour, no IR / EO sensor reference, no pilot quote), no observer
unit identification, no operation name, no speed or altitude estimate, no
weather, no AARO disposition. This is a **process artefact**, not an incident
record. Its evidentiary value is procedural: it shows the OUSD(I&S) ↔ AOR-unit
loop that produces the war.gov UAP-tearline format.

The two underlying observations (10–11 Apr 2025, 2353Z / 0007Z, durations
12 s / 23 s) are extremely short-window contacts only ~14 minutes apart and
across a UTC date boundary — strongly suggestive of a **single sortie** with
two contacts logged under separate tasking-day numbers, by the same airframe.
The mission report this thread references is presumed to exist as a separate
document not included in PURSUE Release 01.

**RU:** Это не доклад миссии и не отчёт об инциденте — это **межведомственная переписка** между аналитиком по раскрытию информации OUSD(I&S) и старшим аналитиком PAROC из 12 AF / DET 3 о том, **на каком уровне секретности** две краткие «выжимки» (tearlines) о событиях с НЛО могут быть опубликованы. Нить переписки документирует рутинный шаг подтверждения рассекречивания, который AARO и поддерживающие его аналитики J2/IO должны выполнять перед публикацией любых сведений об НЛО на war.gov: взять необработанный текст доклада миссии уровня S//NF, запросить у подразделения-оператора (идентификатор скрыт под `1.4(a)`) подтверждение того, что можно огласить, и сформировать tearline-выжимки в открытом виде, очищенные от идентификаторов подразделения, номеров АТО и методологии разведисточников. Оба наблюдения (10–11 апреля 2025 г., 2353Z / 0007Z, длительностью 12 с и 23 с) разделены ~14 минутами через полночь по UTC, что указывает на **один вылет** с двумя зафиксированными контактами. Исходный доклад миссии в PURSUE Release 01 отсутствует.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Document kind — administrative email, not MISREP:** This is one of three "email correspondence" records in PURSUE Release 01 (D50, D51, D52). All three are short (under 150 transcript lines), structurally similar, and share the same OUSD(I&S) Information Disclosure Analyst originator role. They are **process artefacts** of the AARO / DoD UAP-disclosure pipeline, not encounter reports. **RU:** Один из трёх «email-переписок» в PURSUE Release 01 (D50, D51, D52) — все являются артефактами процесса раскрытия, а не отчётами о встречах с НЛО.

- **Distinct from MISREP / Range Fouler structure:** No fixed-field schema — no UAP block, no ACEQUIP block, no Timeline / Takeoff / Landing block, no Operation field, no observer assessment, no unit POC. Free-form email running through three OUSD(I&S) / 12 AF analysts. **RU:** В отличие от структурированных MISREP и форм Range Fouler, документ не имеет фиксированных полей — свободная переписка трёх аналитиков.

- **Redaction patterns:** `1.4(a)` masks on every operational identifier (callsign, sortie identifier, parent unit, asset type, exact location, exact AOR sub-region, internal classification source). `(b)(6)` masks on every personal identifier. The downgraded tearlines are bracketed by literal "//UNCLASSIFIED//" markers. **RU:** `1.4(a)` скрывает все оперативные идентификаторы; `(b)(6)` — личные данные; tearlines заключены в маркеры `//UNCLASSIFIED//`.

- **Tearline format — established AARO / OUSD(I&S) template:** The pattern "**[date] — //UNCLASSIFIED// US AIRCRAFT OBSERVED 1X POSS UAP FOR [N] SECONDS AT [time]Z, FLYING AT UNK ALTITUDE AND UNK SPEED, NO INTERFERENCE WAS NOTED. //UNCLASSIFIED//**" is the default null-fields tearline used when the unit declines to release any phenomenology. Compare D52's tearline which adds shape/duration descriptors. **RU:** Стандартный шаблон tearline AARO/OUSD(I&S) с нулевыми полями феноменологии, используемый когда подразделение отказывается раскрывать детали наблюдения.
