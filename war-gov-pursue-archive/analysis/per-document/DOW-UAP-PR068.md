## Summary / Резюме

A 63-second infrared sensor clip uploaded with an **Intelligence Information Report (IIR) serial number** as the leading title fragment: `IIR 1 666 S0151 23`. In the standard DIA / DoD IIR format, this decodes as: `IIR` (Intelligence Information Report) — `1` (precedence / single-source code) — `666` (originating unit producer designator) — `S0151` (serial number 0151 within the originating series) — `23` (calendar year 2023). The serial encoding tells us PR068 has an actual upstream IIR document associated with it — unlike most R02 PR-clips which appear to be loose end-user uploads. AARO confirms the platform is U.S. military, the AOR is USNORTHCOM, and the timeframe is 2023; the uploader adds "fifth generation aircraft" (F-22A or F-35-family) and "20 Jan 23".

**RU:** Шестидесятитрёхсекундный клип инфракрасного сенсора, загруженный с **серийным номером Информационного доклада разведки (IIR)** в качестве ведущего фрагмента названия: `IIR 1 666 S0151 23`. В стандартном формате DIA / DoD IIR это декодируется как: `IIR` (Intelligence Information Report) — `1` (приоритет / код одно-источника) — `666` (обозначение производящего подразделения) — `S0151` (серийный номер 0151 в исходной серии) — `23` (календарный год 2023). Кодировка серийного номера говорит, что у PR068 есть фактический вышестоящий IIR-документ, связанный с ним — в отличие от большинства R02 PR-клипов, которые выглядят как свободные загрузки конечного пользователя. AARO подтверждает, что платформа — военная США, зона ответственности — USNORTHCOM, временной интервал — 2023; загрузчик добавляет «истребитель пятого поколения» (F-22A или семейство F-35) и «20 янв. 23».

The phenomenology AARO documents is a 31-second continuous tracked event followed by a 31-second partial-tracking event with a second contrast feature: at 00:00–00:31 the sensor keeps one area of contrast in the FOV centre; at 00:32–01:03 that area moves in and out of the top of the frame as the sensor pans; at 00:48 a **second** area of contrast appears upper-right. The two-target presence at 00:48 is the phenomenological key — it places PR068 alongside PR066, PR067 (USCG and USO clips above) and the R01 `DOW-PR34` (INDOPACOM formation) as a **multi-target IR observation**. The IIR-formatted serial and "fifth generation aircraft" framing together suggest PR068 is the **most administratively traceable** entry in the visible R02 PR-cluster.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Only IIR-serial-numbered PR-record observed in R02 PR-cluster so far:** the uploader's title begins with a standard DIA IIR serial — `IIR 1 666 S0151 23` — which implies an actual upstream IIR document exists. Most R02 PR-clips have narrative uploader titles (place / date / morphology) rather than document-serial titles. PR068 is therefore the most administratively traceable R02 PR-entry in the visible range.
- **IIR serial decoded:** `IIR` = Intelligence Information Report (DIA standardised format) — `1` = precedence/source code (typically "1" = SINGLE SOURCE) — `666` = producer designator (the unit number that issued the IIR; "666" should be resolvable against the DIA producer-designator table; without that table the actual originator is unknown) — `S0151` = serial number 0151 within the producer's 2023 series (low serial — early in calendar year, consistent with 20 Jan date) — `23` = calendar year 2023.
- **Fifth-generation framing is operationally specific:** "fifth generation aircraft" narrows the platform set to F-22A Raptor and F-35-family (A/B/C). Both have differentiated IR sensor stacks: F-35's EO-DAS gives spherical IR with the canonical "diamond reticle"; F-22A's AN/AAR-56 MAWS gives different reticle morphology. The published file (not in this stub) likely permits unambiguous identification by reticle.
- **Multi-target confirmation at 00:48 places PR068 in the multi-target subset:** R02 visible-range multi-target IR records: PR050 (4+1 formation), PR067 (≥2 transmedium), PR068 (2 in NORTHCOM). This is a structurally meaningful sub-population — multi-target observations are harder to explain prosaically than single-track observations of the same duration.
- **July 2023 upload date is the earliest in the R02 PR-cluster:** uploads to the classified network for R02 records cluster in May–June 2024; PR068's July 2023 upload predates that by ~11 months. PR067 (May 2024) and PR068 (July 2023) are the two visible-range outliers; PR068 is by far the more extreme outlier.
