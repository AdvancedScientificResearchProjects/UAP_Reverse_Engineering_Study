## Summary / Резюме

A **4-minute-50-second** infrared sensor clip — **by far the longest clip in the visible R02 PR-cluster** and **the only one explicitly framed as a transmedium event** ("in and out of water"). The uploader's title makes three claims that AARO's own narrative neither confirms nor denies: (1) **spherical** target morphology; (2) **multiple** targets ("Multiple Spherical UAP"); (3) the targets exhibit **transmedium behaviour** — Unidentified Submerged Object (USO) crossing the air/water interface near a U.S. submarine [CALLSIGN]. AARO's Video Description records four distinct contrast-feature events across the 4:50 timeline (00:45–00:56, 00:57–01:35, 02:11–03:05, 04:09–04:37) but uses only the lexically neutral "area of contrast" language — never confirming sphericity, multiplicity (beyond pairs of contrast features), or air/water crossing.

Описание видео AARO фиксирует четыре различных события контрастной особенности по таймлайну 4:50 (00:45–00:56, 00:57–01:35, 02:11–03:05, 04:09–04:37), но использует только лексически нейтральный язык «область контраста» — никогда не подтверждая сферичность, множественность (помимо пар контрастных особенностей) или пересечение воздух/вода.

PR067 is structurally **the highest-stakes phenomenology claim** in the visible R02 PR-cluster: a USO ("in and out of water") near a U.S. submarine in March 2022 would be operationally and intelligence-sensitive far beyond a generic CENTCOM IR-sensor clip. The 4:50 duration provides enough sensor footage that frame-by-frame analysis could in principle resolve sphericity and air/water crossing claims. The fact that AARO releases this clip **at all**, while redacting both location and submarine identity, is itself significant — and the absence of any confirmed companion MISREP (no D-record paired) leaves PR067 a phenomenologically rich but provenance-thin orphan within R02.

PR067 is the **single strongest in-PURSUE-corpus phenomenological parallel** to that specific Lazar claim. The cross-archive synthesis should treat PR067 as the key evidentiary bridge for evaluating Lazar's transmedium assertions, whether the evaluation is supportive or skeptical.
