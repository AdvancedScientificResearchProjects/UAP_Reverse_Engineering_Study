## Summary / Резюме

A single-page Range Fouler Debrief filed against an Arabian Gulf ISR sortie at 20:40:00Z on 14 May 2020. The Range Fouler form is a SPEAR (Strategy & Plans Evaluation of Aircraft Reports) submission template — narrower-purpose than a MISREP, designed to capture training-range incursions and unidentified contacts encountered during structured operations. SPEAR explicitly sanitises identifying information for aircrew and squadron at intake — the form preserves the date, time, broad mission descriptor (here: "ISR"), and crew-position narrative, but the originating unit signature is masked.

**Shape checkboxes:** **Round** ticked. All others unchecked.

**Operator narrative (verbatim):**

> **"While preforming an ISR tasking (UL TN/Black Hot/Lin), a solid white object flew through the FOV. There was a temporarily lose of the object but re-acquired shortly there after. The crew was able to follow the object as it appeared to make erratic moments above the water. During the follow, crew was able to obtain 4x zoom on the object but lost the object due to poor track placement. While following, the sensor operator was continuously manipulating the sensor to maintain eyes on the object. This is apparent by the waves of the water in the background being visible and not being visible."**

The narrative provides operationally-significant sensor-stack inferences: **"UL TN/Black Hot/Lin"** is the operator-mode shorthand for an **upward-looking thermal narrow / black-hot polarity / linear gain mapping** — a standard MTS-class EO/IR turret configuration for marine surface tracking. The crew lost the contact then re-acquired it, and ultimately lost the track due to **"poor track placement"** — i.e. operator-pointing error rather than object maneuver.

The narrative's note that the **waves being visible / not visible** is offered as evidence of operator-induced sensor manipulation — one of the few PURSUE records where the operator self-flags a possible artefact in the same form as the report.

## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. The "UL TN/Black Hot/Lin" sensor-mode string is operator shorthand. UL = upward-looking? upper-LOS? Most likely "Upper Left Tactical Narrow" given the Arabian Gulf ISR context. TN = Thermal Narrow. Black Hot = polarity (cold-emissive objects appear bright). Lin = linear gain mapping. Worth confirming via operator debrief.

   **RU:** «UL TN/Black Hot/Lin» — сокращённая запись режима сенсора. UL = вероятно «верхний левый тактический узкий»; TN = тепловой узкий; Black Hot = полярность (холодные объекты светлые); Lin = линейное отображение усиления. Требует уточнения у оператора.

2. The 4× zoom achievement implies the object subtended enough field-of-view to allow optical magnification — i.e. it was not at the limit of detector resolution. Combined with the "solid white object" descriptor (in black-hot polarity meaning the object was *cold*) and the checked **Round** shape, the object is consistent with a small cold sphere over warm water — the same phenomenology as D44/D57 ("round, cold object in IR").

   **RU:** 4-кратное зумирование означает, что объект занимал достаточно пикселей — не на пределе разрешения. «Твёрдый белый» в чёрно-горячей полярности = холодный объект. Круглая форма + холодный + над тёплой водой = та же феноменология, что D44/D57.

3. PR36's video shows ~2 minutes of stable IR track followed by lock-loss at 02:15-02:17. D38's narrative says "lost the object due to poor track placement". Are PR36's 2:17 the entire D38 contact arc, or a curated subset?

   **RU:** PR36 (2:17) — это весь контакт D38 или вырезка? Форма фиксирует только начало (20:40:00Z), без конечного времени. PR36 может быть единственным источником информации о длительности контакта.

4. The operator's self-flagging of sensor-induced erraticism ("the waves of the water in the background being visible and not being visible") is unusual for AARO submission — most Range Fouler narratives commit to object-driven motion. Did SPEAR's intake reviewers credit this self-caveat?

   **RU:** Самооценка оператора о возможном артефакте сенсора нетипична для подачи в AARO — большинство Range Fouler фиксируют объект-обусловленное движение. Учли ли рецензенты SPEAR это примечание?

5. The contact altitude 20,000 ft — is this the friendly-aircraft altitude or the contact altitude? At 20,000 ft, a "round, cold object" with no visible propulsion is consistent with a high-altitude balloon. The "erratic movements above the water" language is more consistent with a low-altitude object.

   **RU:** 20 000 футов — высота дружественного ЛА или контакта? На этой высоте «круглый холодный объект» без видимого движителя совместим с высотным аэростатом, но «хаотичные движения над водой» подразумевают низкую высоту объекта.
