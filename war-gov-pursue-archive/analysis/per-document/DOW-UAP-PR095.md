## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute 49-second infrared sensor clip from a U.S. military platform in USCENTCOM AOR, dated **5 May 2020** by the original uploader with explicit geography "**Gulf of Arabia**" (Arabian Sea / Persian Gulf area). PR095 is the **long-form companion** to the 30-second PR093 — both share the identical uploader-defined title but are distinct DVIDS assets and distinct files. AARO's timeline reports active sensor engagement only in the **first 1 minute 51 seconds**: pan-tracking opens at 00:00–00:16, a zoom-in at 00:17–00:20 reveals "**multiple distinct areas of contrast**" at higher magnification, 00:21–00:35 maintains pan-tracking through further zoom-in (the areas enter and exit the FOV as the sensor pans to keep them in frame), 00:36–00:54 zooms out, 00:55–01:46 zooms in again with the areas of contrast becoming "more distinct at higher magnification" and again entering/exiting the FOV under operator pan, then 01:47–01:51 the areas leave the frame at the lower right edge. The remaining **2 minutes 58 seconds (01:52–04:49) contain no content**.

**RU:** Клип инфракрасного сенсора продолжительностью 4 минуты 49 секунд с военной платформы США в зоне ответственности USCENTCOM, датированный **5 мая 2020** оригинальным загрузчиком с явной географией «**Аравийский залив**» (Аравийское море / Персидский залив). PR095 — **длинная** пара к 30-секундной PR093 — обе разделяют идентичный пользовательский заголовок, но являются различными DVIDS-активами и различными файлами. PR095 is the analytically richer half of the PR093/PR095 pair. Where PR093 (30 s) captures only the briefest engagement window with "several areas of contrast", PR095 (4 min 49 s) provides the **canonical operational record** of the 5 May 2020 Gulf of Arabia encounter: an initial single-area-of-contrast track that, **on zoom-in, resolves into multiple distinct areas of contrast** — i.e. **a resolved multi-object encounter rather than a presumed-single-object track**. This zoom-resolution feature directly supports the uploader's "Dual UAP" two-object framing (though AARO's prose stays at "multiple" rather than committing to a count of two). The very long blank tail (01:52–04:49) is consistent with the operator continuing to record after target loss in case of reacquisition.

## Notes / Замечания

- **Long-form companion to PR093:** PR095 is the deeper-record half of the PR093/PR095 pair. Where PR093 captures only the briefest 30-second snippet, PR095 provides the full operational arc with multiple zoom cycles and explicit zoom-resolution of multi-object structure. Researchers consulting either card should consult both.
- **Second segmentation pattern in R02 PR cluster (after PR057a/b):** the PR093/PR095 pairing introduces a *new* duplicate-record pattern — **identical uploader title, different DVIDS assets, different files**. AARO felt the difference was significant enough to attach an explicit "not duplicates" Comment to both records' descriptions. Whether the pattern recurs elsewhere in the PR050–PR099 range should be tracked.
- **Zoom-resolution multi-object signature is corpus-rare:** the PR095 description's "at higher magnification, the area appears as multiple distinct areas of contrast" is one of the most explicit corpus statements that **a single resolved blob is in fact several closely-spaced features**. This is methodologically valuable because it forces analysts to distinguish optical-resolution effects from physical-spacing claims.
- **Active-engagement-then-long-blank-tail format:** 1 min 51 s of content followed by 2 min 58 s of blank is unusual; the most natural explanation is the operator continuing to record after target exit at 01:51 in case of reacquisition (which did not occur). The total file length matches the operator's recording session, not the duration of UAP visibility.
- **Length class consistent with PR092 and PR094:** at 4 min 49 s, PR095 sits with PR092 (4 min 52 s) and PR094 (4 min 59 s) in the ~5-minute upper-bound length class for R02 PR clips. Three of the four cards in this batch share this length class — suggests a deliberate AARO release-side standard for long-form PR clips.
- **R02 boilerplate / disclaimer:** standard March 6, 2026 House Representatives request preamble, AARO classified-network sourcing, chain-of-custody caveat, and non-analysis disclaimer all present. **Additionally** the AARO Comment paragraph explicitly addressing PR093/PR095 distinctness is appended — this is one of the few R02 PR records with an AARO Comment beyond the standard boilerplate.
