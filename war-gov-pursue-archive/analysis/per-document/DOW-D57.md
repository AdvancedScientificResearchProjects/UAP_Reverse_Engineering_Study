## Summary / Резюме

A USAF or contractor ISR aircraft from squadron designator **1172 ATKS** (the
same originating unit that produces `DOW-D44` exactly 41 days later) tracked a
**round, cold object in IR** for an **8-minute window from 21:09Z to 21:17Z**
on 4 September 2020 over the **Gulf of Aden**, while operating at 23,819 ft
HAT. The contact was characterised in the operator narrative as:

> **Contact at 21:09z to 21:17z 04SEP2020. MGRS location 38P L ... (estimated
> lat/long [redacted (b)(1)1.4a]). While at 23,819 HAT over the Gulf of Aden
> we tracked a round, cold object in IR traveling 168 degrees at 277 mph. It
> made a few abrupt directional changes during the 8 minute contact. Our
> sensor was aimed -39 degrees below our altitude with a slant range of
> 6.1?NM and ground range of 8.81 KM. The IR sensor was set to black hot and
> the object in question was a bright white.**

The structured checkbox fields tick **Round** only; all other shape / attribute
boxes are unticked. Number of contacts in "group" = **1**. EA Indications:
none ticked.

The phenomenology and lexical structure of the narrative paragraph **match
`DOW-D44` (15 Oct 2020) almost word-for-word**: same squadron (1172 ATKS),
same IR polarity ("black hot … bright white"), same phrase "**round, cold
object in IR … abrupt directional changes during the X minute contact**", same
Gulf-of-Aden / Arabian-Sea theatre. Either it is the same operator copy-pasting
from a previous report, or the same crew on consecutive sorties using a
memorised sentence pattern. Either way, **D57 and D44 are not independent
corroboration** in any AARO sense — they should be treated as one observer's
two-contact dataset.

The 8-minute observation window is by Range Fouler standards moderately long
(D44 is just 1 minute). The 277 mph forward speed is moderate. Combined with
**abrupt directional changes** in IR while maintaining a "round, cold"
appearance, this case fits the AARO-tracked **slow-maneuverable-object**
phenomenology slot.

**RU:** Самолёт ISR эскадрильи **1172 ATKS** (то же подразделение, что в D44 41 днём позже) отслеживал **круглый холодный объект в ИК** в течение 8 минут (21:09Z–21:17Z) 4 сентября 2020 г. над Аденским заливом на высоте 23 819 ft HAT. Объект двигался со скоростью 277 миль/ч на курсе 168°, совершил несколько резких смен направления. Сенсор: ИК в режиме «чёрная горячая» (black hot) — объект отображался «ярко-белым» (т.е. значительно холоднее фона). Чекбокс: Round (только). Нарратив почти дословно совпадает с D44 (15 октября 2020 г., та же эскадрилья): фраза «round, cold object in IR … abrupt directional changes during the X minute contact» — **сильнейший лексический отпечаток** в корпусе PURSUE. D57 и D44 **не являются независимой корроборацией** — это один наблюдатель, два события.

## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. **What unit is "1172 ATKS"?** Standard USAF formats do not use 4-digit prefixes (e.g. 12 SOS, 33 SOS, 196 ATKS, 482 ATKS). "1172 ATKS" may be a contractor / task-force designator, OPCON unit, or SPEAR-form data-entry artefact. Stable across D44 and D57. A check against AFCENT / AFSOC / ANG order-of-battle for Sep–Oct 2020 in Gulf of Aden / Arabian Sea is warranted. **RU:** Обозначение нестандартное; может быть контрактором, оперативно подчинённым подразделением или артефактом формы SPEAR. Нужна проверка по документам AFCENT/AFSOC/ANG.

2. **Is D57's "168°/277" wind-cell entry actually the contact's direction-of-travel?** The narrative independently states "traveling 168 degrees at 277 mph" — so the form's wind cell is being repurposed as a contact-direction-of-travel field. **RU:** Значения «168°/277» в поле ветра формы, вероятно, это курс/скорость контакта, а не ветер — оператор перепрофилировал поле.

3. **What did the "round, cold object" actually look like?** 277 mph is too fast for balloons, too slow for conventional missiles (which also present hot IR signatures); cold IR + 277 mph + abrupt directional changes is a narrow slot. Military low-RCS recoverable UAVs (RQ-21, ScanEagle, certain Shahed variants) operate at similar speeds but typically have hotter IR signatures from internal-combustion engines. **RU:** 277 миль/ч — быстрее аэростата, медленнее ракеты; «холодный» в ИК исключает большинство воздушных судов с тепловым двигателем. Узкая феноменологическая ниша.

4. **Why the 41-day cycle between D44 and D57?** Most likely 1172 ATKS was operating regular ISR orbits over the Gulf of Aden / Arabian Sea in late summer / fall 2020, and these are two contacts on different sorties. **RU:** Вероятно, 1172 ATKS вела регулярные ISR-орбиты над Аденским заливом поздним летом — осенью 2020 г., и это два контакта в разных вылетах.

5. **Is there a PR-series video for D57 or D44?** The form notes that display tapes should be uploaded. If video was captured, it has not surfaced in PURSUE Release 01. **RU:** Форма предписывает загрузку display tape (.wmv). Видео в PURSUE Release 01 отсутствует.

6. **Why are D44 and D57 in different release tranches?** D44 in MDR 26-0038 to 26-0046 (27 Mar 2026); D57 in MDR 26-0028 (16 Mar 2026). 11-day-apart MDR processing suggests AARO's release ordering is not strictly by source unit. **RU:** D44 и D57 — разные MDR-транши (разница 11 дней), несмотря на одинаковое подразделение.
