## Notes / Замечания

- **Highest-yield record in the September 2020 sub-cluster:** PR085's ~204 seconds of active sensor tracking vs PR084's 2 seconds and PR087's ~9 seconds total. PR085 is structurally the "main event" of the 05–17 September 2020 trio — the others read as opportunistic capture, PR085 reads as deliberate sustained track.
- **Two-CALLSIGN structural marker:** the duplicated `[CALLSIGN] [CALLSIGN]` in the uploader title is the only such occurrence in the PR084/PR085/PR087 trio. This is the strongest structural-evidence reason to treat PR085 as a multi-platform encounter rather than a single-sensor capture — important for cross-correlation analysis and for assessing whether a paired MISREP D-record might exist with two reporting platforms.
- **Bottom→top ascent followed by held track:** the kinematic profile (slow vertical climb 01:18–02:24 then ~2 minutes of nearly stationary center-frame hold) is characteristic of a near-hovering or slowly-drifting target observed from a moving platform whose own motion compensates for the target's slow drift. Consistent with high-altitude balloons; equally consistent with stationary-hover UAP behaviour. Discrimination requires platform velocity vector.
- **Same-month upload:** the September 2020 upload (incident September 2020) gives PR085 a same-month chain-of-custody, the tightest CoC observed in the R02 PR cluster so far. Together with PR084 (also September 2020 upload), the 16–17 September 2020 pair represents the cleanest provenance window in the R02 PR set.
- **Boilerplate / disclaimer:** standard R02 51-records boilerplate header and AARO non-analysis disclaimer both present. "Many of these materials lack a substantiated chain-of-custody" generic caveat applies despite the tight CoC for this specific record.
