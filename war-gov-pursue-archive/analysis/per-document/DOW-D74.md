## Summary / Резюме

USCENTCOM ISR sortie in support of **Operation INHERENT RESOLVE** — counter-VEO target development over Syria. Aircraft launched at 0217Z on 9 November 2023 from a redacted base; takeoff coords masked, MDS Type / Asset Type / Tail Number / Callsign all `1.4a, 1.4g`. The sortie consisted of a 7-Line dispatch chain: did-not-arrive at ISR 1 (38S MB 48...86...), arrived on ISR 2 at 0431Z–0554Z (37S FU 81...06...), arrived on ISR 3 at 0805Z–1933Z (38S MB 48...86...). Total mission masked under `1.4a`; FMV exploit by redacted DGS. Primary sensor: FMV; Sensors Available: `[1.4g]`. Tasking type: Dynamic.

The ISR work was conventional VEO target development: ISR 2 included a probable VOI (vehicle of interest) match; ISR 3 included a 6-stop follow on probable VOI/POI (person of interest) at a residence, with multiple AIMINT matches and entrance/exit observations of POI-class persons over an ~11-hour orbit.

The **UAP encounter is the standout**. While **RTB at 2153Z** the aircraft observed:

> "1X PROB HC UAP SHAPED AS A BOUNCY BALL COME FROM THE SOUTH AT NEAR CO-ALT. [redacted 1.4a] OBSERVED THE PROB UAP DROP ALTITUDE AND SAFELY PASS THEIR AIRCRAFT WHILE CONSISTANTLY MAINTAINING ~424KN. AFTER 7MIN OF WATCHING, THE PROB UAP BECAME OUT OF RANGE AND [redacted] CARRIED ON THEIR RTB. NO EMISSIONS CAME FROM THE PROB UAP, UAP WAS NOT CONSIDERED A THREAT TO THE AIRCRAFT OR PUBLIC SAFETY, AND THE UAP HAD NO EFFECTS ON THE AIRCREW."

The 424 KN sustained-speed claim with **near-co-altitude pass** and **observed altitude drop with maintained velocity** is a substantive performance claim — a 7-minute track at 424 KN at HC altitude implies ~50 NM of track length, in northern Syrian airspace, on FMV only.

## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. **What is "HC" in "PROB HC UAP"?** Most plausible decode: **High-Confidence**. The aircrew tag means "probable, high-confidence UAP" — i.e. the operator was confident enough to commit to a UAP classification without hedging on sensor artefact. Alternative decodes (Hostile Contact, Helicopter, etc.) are less consistent with the rest of the gentext.

   **RU:** Что означает «HC» в «PROB HC UAP»? Наиболее вероятная расшифровка: **High-Confidence** (высокая уверенность). Альтернативные расшифровки (Hostile Contact, Helicopter) менее согласуются с остальным текстом gentext.

2. **What is the underlying object?** 424 KN (≈488 mph / 785 km/h) with sustained co-altitude flight at HC altitude (Kinetic Altitude `170` — interpretation unclear: 17,000 ft if FL170, or 170 m, or 170 KIAS misfiled into the altitude field) is consistent with subsonic jet aircraft (any commercial / military jet at cruise). The "bouncy ball" *shape* descriptor is unusual for known jet aircraft, but the speed alone is not anomalous. The combination — sphere shape + 424 KN sustained + descended through co-altitude + no emissions + "no propulsion visible" — is the inferential outlier. Without the FMV exploit footage (no PR-series companion identified for this date), the case rests on aircrew narrative only.

   **RU:** Что это за объект? 424 УЗ (≈ 785 км/ч) при устойчивом полёте на крейсерской высоте совместимо с дозвуковыми самолётами. Форма «прыгающего мяча» нетипична для известных ЛА. Комбинация «сфера + 424 УЗ устойчиво + снижение при сохранении скорости + нет излучений» — аномалия. Без FMV-записи дело основано только на нарративе экипажа.

3. **Why "near co-alt" then "drop altitude and safely pass"?** This is *avoiding maneuver* phenomenology — the object came at the friendly aircraft co-altitude, then descended to pass safely. Consistent either with a deliberate non-belligerent maneuver by an intelligent platform, or with parallax of an object on a different trajectory misperceived as "co-altitude". The MISREP records "Under Intelligent Control: NO" — explicit aircrew judgement against intelligence.

   **RU:** Почему сначала «почти на той же высоте», а затем «снизился и прошёл мимо»? Это феноменология уклонения — объект приближался на той же высоте, затем снизился для безопасного прохода. Экипаж зафиксировал «Under Intelligent Control: NO».

4. **No PR-series video pair has been identified for D74 in PURSUE Release 01.** The narrative is rich but unsupported by released sensor footage in the public release.

   **RU:** Для D74 не найдено пары видеозаписи серии PR в составе PURSUE Release 01. Нарратив содержателен, но не подкреплён открытыми сенсорными записями.

5. **Redaction asymmetry on UAP description vs narrative**: the structured "UAP Description" field is masked under `1.4g` ("SHOWED AS [1.4g] [1.4g]"), but the GENTEXT/UAP narrative ("SHAPED AS A BOUNCY BALL") is preserved verbatim. Either the structured-field descriptor contained additional shape / size / color detail that was withheld, or the redaction rules were applied inconsistently between the structured field and the prose narrative.

   **RU:** Асимметрия засекречивания: структурированное поле «UAP Description» скрыто под `1.4g`, но нарративный текст («SHAPED AS A BOUNCY BALL») сохранён дословно. Либо поле содержало дополнительные детали формы/цвета, либо правила засекречивания применялись непоследовательно.
