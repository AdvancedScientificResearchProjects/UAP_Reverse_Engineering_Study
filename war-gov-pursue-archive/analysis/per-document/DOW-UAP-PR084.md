## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute-13-second clip likely derived from a **full-motion video (FMV) camera** aboard a U.S. military platform in CENTCOM AOR on 17 September 2020. PR084 is one of the few R02 PR records where AARO explicitly characterises the source as "full-motion video camera" rather than the more generic "infrared sensor" — implying an EO/visible-band FMV turret rather than the IR-dominant capture mode that dominates the R02 PR050–PR099 cluster. The encounter is extremely short relative to the released file: of the 253-second runtime, AARO describes only a **2-second event** (00:25–00:27): "An area of contrast enters from the lower right-hand side of the screen and exits the screen in the upper left." The remaining ~251 seconds are flagged "No Content."

## Notes / Замечания

- **September 2020 sub-cluster anchor / Якорь сентябрьского 2020 подкластера:** PR084 (17 Sept) + PR085 (16 Sept) + PR087 (05 Sept) form an explicit September 2020 CENTCOM sub-cluster. Combined with the previous batch's October 2020 cluster (PR079–PR083, dated 06/07/14/20 October 2020) and the adjacent August 2020 records PR088/PR089 (31 AUG 2020), this traces a **5–7 week sustained UAP activity window** in a single COCOM AOR — one of the longest unambiguously temporally clustered patterns in the R02 PR set.
- **"Full-motion video camera" attribution is unusual within R02 PR cluster:** the dominant R02 PR description pattern uses "infrared sensor"; PR084 and PR085 both break this to specify FMV camera. Likely interpretation: an EO/visible-band turret (MX-15/-20 class) rather than the IR-only MTS-class typical of CENTCOM 2020 ISR. Implications for platform attribution: more likely a manned MC-12 / U-28A / MQ-9 EO payload than a drone IR-only orbit.
- **Extreme low event-to-runtime ratio:** 2 s of event in 253 s of file = ~0.8 %. This is far below the ~3–10 % typical of even sparse R02 PR records. AARO published the full envelope rather than a clipped highlight — preserving the operator's actual capture context (the FMV orbit was clearly not UAP-targeted; the transit was opportunistic).
- **Diagonal lower-right→upper-left transit:** the 00:25–00:27 trajectory is the most specific geometric detail. Combined with PR085's bottom→top diagonal pan-and-zoom track, the September 2020 sub-cluster shows two distinct kinematic profiles — fast diagonal transit (PR084) vs slow tracked ascent (PR085).
- **Boilerplate / disclaimer:** standard R02 51-records boilerplate header (March 6, 2026 House Representatives request, AARO collection from classified network, "many of these materials lack a substantiated chain-of-custody") and AARO non-analysis disclaimer both present.
