## Summary / Резюме

A 4-minute-51-second single-modality infrared sensor clip that AARO assesses is "likely derived from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform operating within the United States Central Command area of responsibility in 2020." The uploader-assigned title is "NAG UAP 1 Jun 20" — the strings "NAG" (callsign? project? unit fragment?) and the suggested date "1 Jun 20" are user-generated and not corroborated by AARO. The file was uploaded to a classified network in June 2024, four years after the implied capture date. The video description gives a four-stage timeline: an area of contrast enters the FOV at 00:02 and is tracked by sensor pan/zoom until 00:36; reticle lock between 00:37 and 03:37; the area of contrast exits the bottom-left quarter at 03:38; the sensor re-acquires via pan-and-zoom from 03:39 to 04:35; final zoom-cycling from 04:36 to 04:51.

Заголовок, присвоенный загрузчиком, — «NAG UAP 1 Jun 20»; строки «NAG» (позывной? проект? фрагмент названия подразделения?) и предполагаемая дата «1 Jun 20» являются пользовательскими и не подтверждены AARO. Файл был загружен в засекреченную сеть в июне 2024, через четыре года после предполагаемой даты съёмки. Описание видео даёт четырёхэтапную временную шкалу: область контраста входит в поле зрения в 00:02 и отслеживается панорамированием/зумом сенсора до 00:36; захват прицельной маркой между 00:37 и 03:37; область контраста выходит из левого нижнего квадранта в 03:38; повторный захват панорамированием и зумом с 03:39 по 04:35; финальное циклирование зума с 04:36 по 04:51.

PR059 stands out as one of the **longest single-target IR tracks** in the PURSUE corpus (4:51 with effectively continuous track-and-reticle lock for almost three minutes). The user-assigned token "NAG" is the analytically interesting unknown: candidate readings include a callsign fragment, an exercise/project codename, an AFSOC unit slug, or an OCONUS site abbreviation (e.g. Nellis-adjacent, but Nellis is CONUS — flag for open question).

## Notes / Замечания

- **Longest sustained IR track in the R02 PR050+ block / Самый длинный непрерывный ИК-трек в блоке R02 PR050+:** the ~3-minute continuous reticle-lock segment (00:37–03:37) is unusual for the PR series, where most clips are sub-1-minute and end on lock-loss. Track stability of this length argues the platform's autotracker held a coherent contrast signature rather than chasing noise.
- **Provenance gap / Разрыв в провенансе:** 2020 capture → June 2024 classified-network upload. This is the standard R02 pattern flagged in AARO's boilerplate ("many of these materials lack a substantiated chain-of-custody") and weakens any forensic claim about the IR data.
- **"NAG" token is an analyst opportunity / Токен «NAG» — возможность для аналитика:** unlike the bracketed `[CALLSIGN]` redactions in PR060–PR063, the "NAG" string in PR059 was left in the uploader title. This is either a redaction failure or a token that AARO judged non-sensitive. Either way it is a thread to pull on.
- **AARO modality call: "infrared sensor" only / Заявленный AARO режим: только «инфракрасный сенсор»:** unlike PR060–PR063 which AARO assesses as **dual-modality** (FMV + IR, or color + IR), PR059 is single-modality IR. This is consistent with a different platform/sensor suite than the 2021/04/12 cluster.
- **Boilerplate / Стандартный текст:** the standard House-of-Representatives provenance paragraph and non-analysis disclaimer are both present.
