## Summary / Резюме

This is **not** a UAP report. It is an **internal Air Force historical reference volume**: the *official registry* of every major launch from Vandenberg AFB, California, from the first launch on 16 December 1958 through 3 February 2000. Authored and maintained by the 30th Space Wing Office of History (Jeffrey Geiger), it is the canonical reference for which booster, which payload, which agency, which launch facility, and which date applied to ~1,418 launches (1958–1978) plus a further 353 (1979–2000) — totalling 1,771 Vandenberg launches in the headline subtotals.

The structure is purely tabular: (1) a glossary defining all booster, payload, program and command acronyms; (2) a launch-facility guide mapping building numbers to present and former designations; (3) Annual Launch Summary by Command tables (Chart I covers 1958–1978, Chart II covers 1979–2000); (4) Annual Launch Summary by Booster tables for the same intervals; (5) a per-launch chronology with vehicle configuration, payload, status and date.

The reason this document is in PURSUE Release 01 is the same reason DOW-D48 is: it is **historical-baseline data** for the Vandenberg Western Range. Anyone screening modern UAP reports for *"is this a booster anomaly, an ICBM test, a sounding rocket, or a known sub-orbital mission?"* needs exactly this register to do the disambiguation. The cut-off date is February 2000 — the document does not extend into the AARO era.

The document carries no redactions, no UAP fields, no AARO disposition, no declassification banner; it is a pre-AARO unclassified Air-Force-history publication included in the PURSUE corpus as historical context.

**RU:** Этот документ — **не доклад о НЛО**. Это официальный исторический реестр ВВС США: сводный перечень всех крупных пусков с авиабазы Ванденберг с 16 декабря 1958 г. по 3 февраля 2000 г. Автор: Джеффри Гейгер (Исторический отдел 30-го Космического крыла). Охватывает ~1 771 пуск (всего): данные по носителям, полезным нагрузкам, ведомствам, стартовым площадкам и датам.

Включён в PURSUE Release 01 с той же целью, что и D48: **историческая база данных** для аналитиков AARO, позволяющая разграничить «аномалию ракеты-носителя» и «реальный НЛО». Граница данных — февраль 2000 г. Нет засекреченных разделов, нет полей НЛО, нет заключений AARO.
