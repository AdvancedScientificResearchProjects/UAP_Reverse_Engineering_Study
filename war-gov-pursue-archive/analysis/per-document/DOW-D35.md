## Summary / Резюме

A 33 SOS / 27 SOW special-mission ISR sortie launched from Larisa AB (LGLR, Greece) at 1504Z on 28 October 2023 — **the day after [`DOW-D33`](DOW-D33.md)'s 270035Z sortie** out of the same base — in support of a tasking masked under `1.4a`. The sortie was a 20h01m round-trip back to LGLR, with on-station 2018Z–0542Z (9h24m) over the fragged target SP at MGRS `36SYC4…[masked]` (south-eastern Mediterranean / north-eastern Egypt area). Asset was 7-lined at 1618Z but did not arrive on-station until 2018Z; performed FMV/SIGINT collection at SP. POL (pattern-of-life) on several COIs from 2019Z–2243Z, no EEI activity; at 2244Z observed 6× vehicles on parking-garage rooftop and no personnel; continued POL through RTB at 0542Z.

While **on RTB at 290811Z** (almost exactly 3 hours after off-station, in the LGLR-bound transit corridor across the Aegean), the asset observed **1× POSS UAP** at MGRS `35SMV3…[masked]`. The narrative is verbatim:

> **"AT 0811Z, [redacted] WAS RTB WHEN THEY SPOTTED A UAP FLYING JUST ABOVE THE SURFACE OF THE OCEAN WATER. THE UAP FLEW STRAIGHT ABOVE THE OCEAN TOWARDS LANDS. AT 0811Z, [redacted] LOST THE UAP FROM THEIR FEED."**

UAP Description: **"SEEMINGLY CIRCULAR, TOO SMALL TO MAKE OUT DETAILS"** — the *exact same operator-supplied description string* used in [`DOW-D33`](DOW-D33.md). UAP Maneuverability Observations: NONE. UAP Physical State: Solid. UAP Under Intelligent Control: NO. UAP Signatures: NONE. Observer Assessment of UAP: Benign. Kinetic Velocity: **30 MPH**. UAP first and last coords are in the same MGRS 1-km square — the entire observation arc happened inside one digit-block at very low forward velocity.

The contact-window in the GENTEXT/UAP block is reported as **a single 0811Z timestamp** for both initial detection and lock-loss — i.e. **a sub-minute observation**. (D33's equivalent encounter ran 0035Z–0038Z, ~3 minutes.) FMV exploited by GET. After landing 1105Z LGLR, total mission 20:01, FMV 09:24, 1× FMV tasking prosecuted.

**RU:** Разведывательный вылет 33 SOS / 27 SOW с LGLR (Ларисса, Греция) в 1504Z 28 октября 2023 г. — на следующий день после вылета D33. Задание засекречено (`1.4a`). Маршрут: LGLR → SP `36SYC4...` (ЮВ Средиземноморье) → LGLR; 20 ч 01 мин; на задании 2018Z–0542Z (9 ч 24 мин). Наблюдение за объектами интереса (COIs), без EEI-активности.

На **RTB в 290811Z** — **1× POSS UAP** в районе MGRS `35SMV3...`. Нарратив MISREP дословно:

> **"AT 0811Z, [redacted] WAS RTB WHEN THEY SPOTTED A UAP FLYING JUST ABOVE THE SURFACE OF THE OCEAN WATER. THE UAP FLEW STRAIGHT ABOVE THE OCEAN TOWARDS LANDS. AT 0811Z, [redacted] LOST THE UAP FROM THEIR FEED."**

Описание: **«SEEMINGLY CIRCULAR, TOO SMALL TO MAKE OUT DETAILS»** — дословно идентично D33. Манёвры — NONE; физическое состояние — твёрдый; под разумным управлением — НЕТ; сигнатуры — NONE; оценка — безопасный; кинетическая скорость — **30 MPH**. Первая и последняя координаты совпадают — весь трек в одном километровом квадрате. Контакт — менее одной минуты. ИК-ролик PR35 (24 с) — парный документ.

## Open questions / Открытые вопросы

1. **Same object, two sorties?** D33 and D35 sorties are 2 days apart, both out of LGLR, both AFSOC ISR, both reporting "circular, too small to make out details" UAP "flying just above the surface of the ocean water" in the same broader Eastern-Mediterranean / Aegean theatre. The MGRS grids differ (~150 km apart based on ZE/ZF column letters). Could be the same drift-driven object observed twice, or two distinct objects of the same morphology class.

   **RU:** Один ли это объект, наблюдавшийся дважды? D33 и D35 — 2 дня разницы, та же база, тот же театр, идентичные нарратив и описание. MGRS отличаются (~150 км между квадратами). Возможно один объект (дрейф) или два объекта одного класса.

2. **Sub-minute contact window:** D35 records both first-contact and lock-loss at the same 0811Z timestamp. PR35 (24 s) is the IR-video record; was the 24-second video the entire contact, or a curated subset? D33's PR34 was 2:57 — about the same as the 3-minute D33 contact window.

   **RU:** Наблюдение менее одной минуты: обе метки — 0811Z. PR35 (24 с) — это весь контакт или вырезка? PR34 (D33) длится 2:57 ≈ 3 мин контакту D33.

3. **Verbatim description re-use:** "SEEMINGLY CIRCULAR, TOO SMALL TO MAKE OUT DETAILS" is identical between D33 and D35. Is the operator copy-pasting from a previous report or genuinely observing identical morphology?

   **RU:** Дословное совпадение описания D33 и D35: шаблонный копипаст или реально идентичная морфология? При той же организации и 2-дневном окне оба объяснения возможны.

4. The "TOWARDS LANDS" direction in D35 is operationally relevant — the UAP was tracked moving from sea toward landfall. PR35's AARO description confirms ("flying near the surface of the ocean toward land"). Did the asset attempt to hand off the track to ground assets at the landfall point?

   **RU:** «TOWARDS LANDS» (опечатка «LAND») — направление в сторону суши. PR35 подтверждает («toward land»). Была ли попытка передать трек наземным средствам?

5. D35 carries 4× Hellfire variants (2× AGM-114R9E + 2× AGM-114R2) on the wings — the only 33 SOS LGLR record with weapons explicitly itemised. Suggests the airframe was configured for armed-overwatch, but weapons were not employed against the UAP.

   **RU:** D35 несёт 4 ракеты Hellfire — единственная запись 33 SOS с явным перечислением вооружения. Платформа была настроена для вооружённого сопровождения, но оружие против НЛО не применялось.
