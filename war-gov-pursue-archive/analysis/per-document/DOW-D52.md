## Summary / Резюме

This is an **OUSD(I&S) ↔ 15 AF / DET 1 PAROC** email exchange concerning an
extension request on a previously-approved UAP tearline — the originally-cleared
text covered the **month and day** of the underlying incident, and the
OUSD(I&S) Information Disclosure Analyst writes back asking the unit to
**also approve including the year**. The PAROC Intel Data Analysis Technician
agrees, and provides the year-inclusive tearline:

> **31 OCT 24, U.S Aircraft observed a possible UAP. It appeared to be
> oval/orb shaped, likely moving at a low speed. The U.S Aircraft had eyes on
> the poss UAP for over 2 hours.**

The two-hour-plus contact duration is a striking phenomenological marker: it is
**by far the longest single observation window in the modern Department-of-War
PURSUE tranche**, dwarfing the typical CENTCOM MISREP windows of 1–60 minutes
and the Range-Fouler windows of 1–8 minutes. A 2-hour FMV-track of an
"oval/orb shaped, likely moving at a low speed" object is consistent with a
long-loiter ISR airframe (U-28A Draco, MC-12W Liberty, MQ-9 Reaper,
RC-26B/C/D, P-8A) that maintained a stable orbit relative to the contact.

What the document does *not* contain: aircraft type, callsign, sortie
identifier, exact location, exact AOR (the unit declined to release AOR
attribution), sensor used, observer assessment, AARO disposition, follow-on
action. The purpose of the document is the **tearline year-inclusion approval
workflow**, not the encounter narrative.

The August 2024 vs October 2024 date inconsistency in the catalog entry is
unexplained — the most likely reading is that the email thread had an earlier
phase (perhaps August 2024) approving the original month/day-only tearline,
followed by this October-or-later phase approving the year addition.

**RU:** Это переписка OUSD(I&S) ↔ PAROC 15 AF/DET 1 о **расширении ранее одобренной tearline-выжимки**: исходно утверждены месяц и день инцидента, в данном обмене запрошено и одобрено добавление года. Итоговая tearline: «31 октября 2024 г. воздушное судно США наблюдало возможный НЛО. Объект имел форму овала/шара, предположительно двигался с малой скоростью. Воздушное судно США отслеживало возможный НЛО более 2 часов». **Продолжительность наблюдения более 2 часов — наибольший одиночный временной окон в современном трансе PURSUE**, характерный для долгоипящего ISR-воздушного судна. AOR не раскрыт, несмотря на 15 AF/DET 1 = AFCENT-ориентация. Несоответствие дат в каталоге («август 2024» в названии vs. «31 октября 2024» в тексте) предположительно отражает двухэтапный процесс согласования tearline.

2. **What AOR did the unit decline to release?** 15 AF / DET 1's natural AOR is AFCENT (CENTCOM). Possible reasons for withholding: (a) covert orbit location, (b) adversary inference about ISR coverage on a sensitive day, (c) the platform was on temporary AFRICOM / EUCOM tasking. **RU:** Почему AOR засекречен? 3. **What platform held a 2-hour+ contact?** Long-loiter ISR is the obvious candidate: U-28A Draco, MC-12W Liberty, MQ-9 Reaper Block 5, RC-26B, P-8A Poseidon. A fast-jet cannot maintain 2-hour contact on a slow-moving target. **RU:** 2-часовой контакт характерен для долгоипящего ISR: U-28A, MC-12W, MQ-9, RC-26B, P-8A. 6. **Why the year-inclusion two-step?** Standard AARO / DoD UAP disclosure practice is to release date with year by default.
