**EN:** A LinkedIn post (and referenced 13-page in-house report) by Robert Cardillo (NSWCIHD) summarising an investigation into a sustained 17-night window of aerial incursions over **Langley Air Force Base in December 2023**, during which multiple unidentified systems operated within restricted military airspace. The investigation classifies the objects as "data-bearing aerospace artifacts" rather than UAP, applies the author's own **Gabriella Meta-Architecture** systems-engineering framework with two derivative protocols — the **Unified Constraint Engine (UCE)** and **Tri-Layer Falsification Protocol (TLFP)** — and integrates ~90 minutes of eyewitness video from Jonathan Butner with "quantum-imaging forensics".

The investigation categorises the intruders into **two distinct types**:
- **Class I orbs** — car-sized, silent, observed in synchronised "conveyor-belt" formations.
- **Class II craft** — larger saucer-shaped, ~20–30 ft diameter, theorised as command-and-control node.

The propulsion theory advanced is the **Scalar Resonant Mobility System (SRMS)** — silent high-acceleration motion via "resonance with a four-dimensional vacuum lattice", explicitly contrasted with reaction-mass thrust. Recommendations include declassifying NASA sensor data, modernising domestic sensor arrays, and implementing an "Irreversibility Firewall (IFW)" to preserve human authority within future autonomous-defence protocols. Conventional explanations (hobbyist drones, commercial ISR platforms, atmospheric phenomena) are stated as falsified, though the underlying 13-page report is not publicly linked from the LinkedIn post and the methodology acronyms (Gabriella, UCE, TLFP, SRMS, IFW) appear to be the author's own coinage rather than established field terminology.
