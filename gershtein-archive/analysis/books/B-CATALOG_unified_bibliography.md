## Method

Eight catalog source pages were ingested and scanned for entries authored or co-authored by Gershtein. Bibliographic fields (title, year, publisher, ISBN, co-authors, format/pages) were merged across sources. Russian titles preserved verbatim. The National Library of Belarus (NLB) record provides the most complete formal bibliographic descriptions; LiveLib provides the most complete reader-facing list; bookvoed adds a few retail-only titles; UfoKom uniquely records the 1996 КИМЯ booklet; lib-dpr adds the only ISBN we have (НЛО в секретных архивах ФБР). AST publisher pages were 404 / search-fallback only.

- **lib-dpr** returned a 23 KB stub (single-book record at the Donetsk Republican Library Krupskaya). The single record happens to be the only ISBN-bearing entry in the entire 8-source corpus.

- **ufo-com.net** is the only source that records the 1996 КИМЯ booklet, which appears to be Gershtein's first published work.
