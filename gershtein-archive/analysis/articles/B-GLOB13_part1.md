## 5. The catalogue legend — table-column semantics

### 5.1 Legend row (line 58-60)

The summary table opens with the column headers:

> «Country | Title | Size (Mb) | Total number of PDF pages | Number of PDF files | Number of other files | Authors (more than 4 publications) | Bulletins (more than 1)»

> **EN expansion:**
> 1. **Country** — alphabetical by English country name.
> 2. **Title** — name of the holding organisation, journal, or government body.
> 3. **Size (Mb)** — disk footprint of the holding inside Kalytyuk-Gershtein's archive.
> 4. **Total number of PDF pages** — page count summed across all PDFs in that holding.
> 5. **Number of PDF files** — discrete PDF count.
> 6. **Number of other files** — non-PDF artefacts (DOC, image, text, audio).
> 7. **Authors (more than 4 publications)** — only contributors with >4 pieces are named; lesser contributors collapse to «-».
> 8. **Bulletins (more than 1)** — only periodicals with >1 issue indexed are named.

This 8-column schema is the **structural framework** of the entire compendium and recurs as a header every ~30 country-rows where the PDF page break occurs (e.g. lines 137-141, 222-224, 306-308, 387-389, 471-473, 554-556).

### 5.2 The "more-than-4-publications" rule

> **Methodological inference:** The author-naming threshold (>4 publications) and bulletin-naming threshold (>1 issue) function as **selection filters**. Most cells are «-» (empty) because most holdings do not meet the threshold. This is an editorial discipline, not under-recording.
