**RU** «Уфология как таковая зашла сейчас в очень серьезный методический тупик. И выйти из него можно только двумя способами. Первый способ это дождаться какого-то дальнейшего развития событий... Но есть еще и другой вариант, в настоящее время более доступный и не предполагающий пассивного ожидания. Это обращение к историческому аспекту уфологии.»
**EN:** "Ufology as such has now run into a very serious methodological dead-end. There are only two ways out. The first is to wait for some further development of events… But there is another way, currently more accessible and not requiring passive waiting — turning to the historical aspect of ufology."

### 2.20 Ball-lightning hypothesis as overloaded death-spirit

**EN:** "Among ball-lightning hypotheses there is one that ball lightning is such an entity — a previously invisible 'spirit' that, hit by a lightning discharge, received an energy overload and dies in that luminescence. **EN:** "In the vast Universe a civilisation perishes somewhere all the time. What if some UFOs were once real ships, but then perished, and we now see their ghosts — just as we see phantom cars, phantom carriages, phantom ships, phantom aeroplanes and so on?"

### 2.23 "Witches as glowing balls" — modern Ghana ethnography

### 2.24 The "old crippled woman = chased witchcraft ball" anecdote
