## SECTION 1 — Modern UAP transition: terminology, scientific journals, Carter precedent

**EN:** "Ufology is indeed on the rise now, and it's a very interesting rise — because as soon as it was recognised at the highest level both in the United States and Western Europe, the theme — which previously was considered laughable and not respectable to discuss — became something else."

**EN:** "In the US, as is known, an overwhelming majority of the population believes in the existence of UFOs and that the government hides the truth from citizens. When the electoral cycle begins, all candidates start pedalling the UFO theme — 'I'll come to power, I'll open all the archives.'"

**EN:** "In the past, terminology was very often changed to somehow legitimise research. So it was in the Soviet Union, when instead of the supposedly discredited term 'UFO' they began to use the term 'anomalous phenomena.' And immediately everything became permissible, decent, and good."

**EN:** "In the 19th century the medical community used to mock animal magnetism — charlatanism, not accepted, not examined. When they discovered the ridiculed animal magnetism worked, they renamed it hypnosis, and immediately everything became scientific, became researchable, scientific schools studying hypnosis appeared."

**RU** «Но, в принципе, основные методологические вопросы, они как были при картере, так они сейчас и остались. То есть, они не были разрешены, они не получили разрешения. **EN:** "Fundamentally, the basic methodological questions stand as they did under Carter — they have not been resolved. New instrumentation only increases, roughly speaking, the weight and credibility of testimony. But methodologically nothing has changed yet."

### 1.12 Why the recognition shift — accumulated critical mass

**RU** «когда его очень долго замалчивают, то накапливается критическая масса сообщений, данных, случаев. Причем не случаев в стиле дяди Васи или Джон Доу, в пьяном виде, лежа на обочине наблюдал. А когда наблюдают военные летчики, когда наблюдают профессионалы, располагающие хорошей аппаратурой»
**EN:** "When long suppressed, a critical mass of reports, data, cases accumulates — not 'Uncle Vasya' or John Doe lying drunk on the roadside, but military pilots, professionals with good instrumentation."
